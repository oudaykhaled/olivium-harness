# Declared conflicts: exact-revision admission

**User benefit.** A policy owner can declare incompatible approved instructions, choose the applicable revision, and see a precise blocker until that choice is recorded. This does not infer semantic conflict or grant goals policy authority.

**Code basis.** `Evidence.create_claim/1` stores immutable citation-derived hashes; `Decisions.decide/2` approves exact revisions. `Company.create_plan/1` snapshots approved claims; `decide_plan/2` and `build_inputs/1` check eligibility. `Harnesses.build_plan/2` repeats admission; `Harnesses.eligible/1` gates export. `Harnesses.specification/3` emits selected claims with `goal_id: nil`. `priv/schemas/records-v1alpha1.json` names `conflict_ids`, but no resolution persists.

**Proposed scope.** Add workspace-scoped, append-only conflict declarations naming two distinct exact claim IDs and revision hashes, an actor, reason and audit event. Add one immutable resolution per declaration naming the winning revision, actor and expected declaration hash. A rejected or superseded claim, cross-workspace reference, duplicate pair, or stale hash fails admission. A plan snapshot pins all applicable declarations and resolutions for its selected claims. A declared pair with both members selected, or with a selected member and no current resolution, blocks plan approval, build and export with an actionable conflict ID. The chosen loser must be absent from the plan. Declaration after prior approval makes the affected plan ineligible for build/export until a successor is proposed and approved; historical plan and artifact bytes remain intact. Preserve existing behavior for plans with no applicable declaration.

**Falsifiable acceptance.** Tests show two approved contradictory fixture claims compile before declaration; after declaration, approval/build/export refuse the unresolved pair. Exact resolution plus a successor selecting only the winner succeeds. A changed/superseded revision or new declaration invalidates the earlier snapshot. Unrelated plans stay eligible; purge/restore and legacy artifact verification retain existing guarantees. Browser/API copy identifies the pair and safe next action without implying automatic detection.

**Risk and alternatives.** Declarations can affect existing plans: index by workspace and exact refs, audit writes, and verify every gate. Goal-origin rules require a separate authority model. Exact-artifact qualification readiness offers evaluation guidance but cannot prevent a known contradiction from compiling.

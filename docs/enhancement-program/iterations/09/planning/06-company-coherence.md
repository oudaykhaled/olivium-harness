# 09/06 — Cross-source company coherence

**Recommendation / ADR.** Add bounded **assisted synthesis** for two or three selected current company sources and one saved future goal. Return at most three review cards: recurring practice or failure mode, discussion/decision evidence, and conflicting or newer guidance marked “unresolved.” Each card proposes a rule, check or evaluation criterion linked to the goal. Discussion alone stays tentative. The operator edits or rejects cards, approves exact claim revisions, then composes/exports through existing gates. This is no semantic certification.

**Current seams.** `Models.propose/2` sends only one latest revision and persists its validated output as proposed claims (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:150-184`). Its citation validator is tied to that one source (`lib/harness_forge/models/proposal.ex:49-55,109-124`). `Models.infer/2` already enforces selected model, consent, source eligibility, metered budget and bounded output (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:89-145,229-258`), but its server-owned profiles permit only proposal/chat (`lib/harness_forge/models/profile.ex:1-51`). Existing claims accept cited revisions and exact human decisions (`lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex:289-325`; `lib/harness_forge_web/live/portal_live.ex:407-419`). Company plan approval and compiler remain separate (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:110-136`; `lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:70-90`).

**Boundary and ownership.** One Elixir engineer adds a finite `company_synthesis` profile and validator for current source/revision/UTF-8 byte citations, uncertainty and goal hash. A second author adds portal review/edit/save using existing claim/decision paths; root integrates plan/export. At most three authors, likely two PRs of 200–350 net lines each. Preserve consent, budgets, purge and immutable revisions. Model unavailability stays visible.

**Acceptance/falsifier.** Positive: a reproducible synthetic corpus with two sources showing a repeated incident and a later decision produces a cited, editable proposal tied to the selected goal; human approval leads to a company-specific exported artifact. Negative: conflicting guidance is visibly unresolved and cannot silently become an approved rule; rejected proposal does not enter plan/export. Edge: changed/revoked source or goal before save/approval blocks stale evidence; repeated citations do not imply independent support. Falsifier: fabricated offsets, old revision, discussion mislabeled as decision, or any proposal entering export without exact approval.

**Alternative.** Deterministic retrieval plus manual synthesis reduces model cost and false confidence but leaves the central cross-source reasoning task with the operator. Assisted synthesis has variable quality; synthetic tests prove plumbing and refusal only. Real-company/model quality requires separate owner review.

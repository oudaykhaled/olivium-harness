# 11 — Replayable company analysis review

**User benefit:** An operator reopens the exact cross-source analysis they reviewed, sees supporting document/goal revisions, then edits/rejects/approves proposals before company-specific export. Today `Models.propose/2` processes one latest source revision (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:150-183`); `Evidence.citation/3` binds ranges in one eligible revision (`lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex:272-289`). Company plans pin selected claims/goals (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:72-86,241-261`). No reviewed analysis spans documents, plans and history. Current acceptance proves synthetic export with static evaluation, which cannot qualify release (`test/harness_forge/company_acceptance_test.exs:110-153`).

**Decision candidate:** Persist an immutable *analysis candidate* with source/revision and goal hashes, actual model/config identity, and typed findings: recurring practice/failure, decision versus discussion, exception, changed/conflicting guidance, goal link, proposed rule/skill/check/evaluation criterion, citations and uncertainty. Findings remain proposed. Portal reopens the candidate for correction and individual rejection/approval; existing Company plan/build/export binds approved exact revisions. Stale inputs invalidate affected findings. Principal Elixir owns candidate persistence; root owns migration/portal integration; QA owns corpus/oracles. Estimate: two author days plus review. Storage and review effort buy repeatability.

**Falsifiable oracle:** Use a public synthetic corpus: two policy revisions, meeting decision, two incidents repeating a failure, exception, conflicting old instruction and future goal. Positive: a synthetic provider response yields cited failure, decision, conflict and goal-linked check candidates; operator edits one, rejects another and approves the corrected revision. Exported target files contain the approved correction and provenance, excluding the rejected and pre-edit text. Negative: discussion cannot become decision; rejected/stale findings never compile. Edge: one changed source invalidates only dependent findings; unrelated approvals survive, with stable hashes and no recomputation. Reopen shows reviewed bytes. Falsifier: uncited/unapproved text reaches export.

**Evidence limit:** Controlled response and corpus test routing/provenance/review, not semantic recall or real-company/model quality. If local inference, connector scope or isolated runtime is unavailable, show its exact unavailable state; human authoring remains distinct from AI analysis/model-backed evaluation (`lib/harness_forge/readiness.ex:29-35`). No paid fallback. A coverage gate costs less but misses the cross-source operator outcome.

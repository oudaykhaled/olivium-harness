# 03 — Cross-source candidate admission before insertion

**Benefit.** An operator reviews a proposed company rule grounded in two documents, edits or rejects it, then includes only an approved revision in a company-specific exported harness. Admission prevents invented provenance from becoming a plausible review item.

**Context.** `Models.propose/2` sends one current revision; `Models.Proposal.validate/2` requires same-revision exact UTF-8 citations (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:150–190`; `lib/harness_forge/models/proposal.ex:70–125`). Persistence wraps citation/claim writes in one transaction (`proposal.ex:83–119`). `Evidence.create_claim/1` supports immutable successors; `Decisions.decide/2` binds explicit decisions to revision hashes (`lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex:289–329`; `lib/harness_forge/decisions.ex:7–48`). Company selects approved claims; export rechecks eligibility (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:264–276`; `lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:122–165`). Tests cover only one-source synthetic proposals and forged-span refusal (`test/harness_forge/models_test.exs:70–130`).

**Decision candidate.** Extend proposal input to bounded, explicitly selected current revisions. Require each cross-source candidate to cite two distinct selected revisions with exact spans. Ask the model to distinguish observed practice from discussion, decisions, exceptions and conflicting/outdated guidance; the operator judges those semantic claims. Validate structural provenance before any claim/citation/audit insert; recheck freshness inside the transaction. Permit partial analysis: milestone/goal coverage belongs to plan review. A human edits by successor revision, rejects or explicitly approves before Company selection.

**Contract.** Positive: synthetic history and policy documents yield a cited, uncertainty-marked proposal; operator edits and approves its successor, selects it with a future goal and milestone acceptance, approves the plan, then exports. Verify its revision/statement in exported `AGENTS.md` and provenance (`lib/harness_forge/adapters/opencode.ex:262–271`; `lib/harness_forge/compiler.ex:85–131`). Negative: unselected-source citation or fabricated span rejects the batch with zero proposal/audit inserts. Edge: changed source before persistence returns stale-source; rejected/superseded candidates are absent from exported rules; historical approved artifacts keep their hashes.

**Tradeoff/falsifier.** Models/Evidence and portal authors, with root integrating Company/export, roughly 2–4 days plus synthetic integration/browser tests. Approval-only provenance checks leave forged-looking review items; creation-time goal coverage blocks partial analysis. Any malformed candidate persisted, auto-approval, or exported unapproved revision falsifies this contract. Fixtures cannot establish real-company semantic quality.

## Connected change and narrow contracts

Keep the 25-row/4,096-byte inspector. Add server-owned corpus membership and a scan job with a persisted cursor, source generation, per-file outcome and idempotency key. **Proposed initial bounds to test:** one active scan per workspace; at most 100 files or 8 MiB of accepted text per commit; at most two queued commit batches; one SQLite writer; a bounded retry/backoff and cancellation state. Commit revision, membership, passage-index outbox and checkpoint atomically. An interrupted or incomplete scan stays *incomplete*, never implying deleted files. Expose distinct discovered, eligible, captured, indexed, excluded, failed, stale and access-unverified counts; report physical files walked separately from preseeded logical records ([study](../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/STUDY.md#L81-L89)).

For one saved goal, plan scoped FTS5 candidates first, cap the candidate set at 100 and hydrate at most 24 exact passages plus bounded parent context. Reserve contrary/unknown slots and abstain when a critical domain is absent or a limit truncates coverage. Persist the versioned snapshot and cited, uncited and transitive dependencies before human edit/rejection, exact claim approval and separate plan approval; recheck it through corrected target export and representative evaluation. These are starting budgets, not quality thresholds ([study](../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/STUDY.md#L141-L153), [handoff](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L91-L105)).

## Reuse, acceptance and limits

Use existing `CorpusReview` paging, SQLite/FTS and Oban; attach pinned Graphiti 0.31.2 search behind the same nonempty server scope and evidence budget. Its empty group list becomes unfiltered, so refuse it before dispatch ([pinned `search.py`](../../../../../../research/harnessforge-graph-rag-study-2026-11-02/sources/graphiti/graphiti_core/search/search.py#L101-L166)). Before implementation, freeze one public corpus, questions, critical-evidence oracle, thresholds and FTS baseline plan. Compare FTS and Graphiti afterward on identical snapshots, warm/cold state, hardware, concurrency and ACL checks; record SQL counts, bytes hydrated, queue age, checkpoint lag, RSS, p50 and sample p95/p99 only with adequate repeated observations. Set a proposed ≤100 SQL-call Portal mount budget on matched 500/5,000-record fixtures; profile loaders before accepting it. Positive: >500 physical files resume, >100 members, contrary passage, corrected exported bytes. Negative/recovery: empty scope, queue saturation, crash, stale revision, failed purge and incomplete scan deny misleading output. The 51 offline passes and million *logical* repetitive probe prove neither physical capture nor installed backend, semantic quality or production percentiles ([reuse decision](../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/REUSE-DECISION.md#L93-L120), [study](../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/STUDY.md#L252-L267)). Rough effort: 4–7 engineering days plus 2–3 days of matched evaluation; defer larger limits until measured.

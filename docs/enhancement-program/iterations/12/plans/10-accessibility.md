# Accessible corpus scope and conflict review

**User benefit.** A keyboard and screen-reader user should select an authorised corpus and saved goal, understand what was captured or excluded, inspect supporting and conflicting passages, and deliberately correct, reject or approve a recommendation without traversing a company-sized checklist. The UI should show one bounded reviewable retrieval snapshot, not imply that every company file was analysed.

**Observed seam.** Frozen `components/company/analysis.ex:19–67,195–320` pins 2–4 checked source revisions and renders every assigned source with a checkbox, then one goal select. `catalog.ex:80–105,429–444,517–533` repeats source/goal checkbox membership. These become unusable as the corpus grows. The current report already distinguishes citation coverage from completeness, renders excerpts, and asks for a human conflict resolution (`analysis.ex:453–543`); its open findings disclosure offers a useful review pattern. `company_panel.ex:253–270,1218–1237` owns disclosure and focus events. `assets/js/app.js:140–165` focuses only fixed IDs when visible. The predecessor/successor controls are inside closed `<details>` (`assessment.ex:529–581`), the retained WEB-11-01 gap. CSS offers visible company focus and a one-column mobile layout (`assets/css/app.css:165–187,250–273`), while full keyboard, screen-reader, contrast and novice outcomes remain unmeasured (iteration 11 UI/UX retrospective).

**Proposal and ownership.** Extend the existing Company Guide/Catalog/Analysis presentation with a labelled corpus-scope control and saved-goal selector, paged metadata rows, a concise inclusion/exclusion/progress summary, and a bounded evidence list. Each result needs a plain-language supporting/conflicting/unknown label, source title, documentary status, effective versus capture date, parent context, exact revision/hash/UTF-8 span, and a separate expandable identity block. Show missing/blocked/stale counts and abstention before an approval control; use ordinary buttons, headings, fieldsets and `aria-describedby`, with focus sent to a visible status/error heading after each LiveView patch. Keep contradictory passages adjacent to the proposed guidance and the human resolution field. Move successor guidance outside the closed disclosure. The CompanyPanel/PortalLive owner handles navigation and focus; `assets/js/app.js` and `assets/css/app.css` handle visible target checks and 320 CSS-pixel/200% reflow. The backend owner supplies server-derived nonempty access scope, bounded membership/page tokens and snapshot status; Elixir/SQLite/FTS5 stay authoritative, with pinned Graphiti 0.31.2/Jev through a bounded Python adapter in the following functionality slice.

**Acceptance.** Positive: with a larger synthetic corpus and one goal, keyboard-only review reaches a paged conflict, opens its bounded passage/context, saves correction, and reaches exact Evidence/plan decisions and corrected export. Negative: foreign-scope, missing-page, revoked or incomplete capture never appears as eligible evidence or an enabled approval; unknown coverage is announced. Recovery: stale snapshot or failed retrieval retains the chosen goal, reports what changed and focuses a visible retry/reselect action; revoked derivatives remain blocked through failed summary rebuild. Use Playwright 1.49+ role/label locators, keyboard and axe checks at 320/390 CSS pixels and 100/200% zoom, plus source-backed LiveView assertions. Estimate **two UI author-days plus one backend seam day**, excluding the subsequent retrieval integration.

**Dissent.** A compact conflict panel over the present four-source contract could ship sooner, but leaves the main manual bottleneck intact. A whole-corpus multiselect would preserve familiar controls while multiplying focus stops and payload size. Prefer paged scope and bounded passage hydration; measure missed critical evidence and reviewer errors before treating screen success as company-harness quality.

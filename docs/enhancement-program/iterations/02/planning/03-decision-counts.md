# Decision counts: show both review queues

**Observed defect.** The saved Company browser baseline displays “AWAITING A DECISION 0” beside a proposed 0.2.0 plan (`tmp/enhancement-program/01/browser/company-dom.txt:61,235–236`). `PortalLive.proposed/1` counts only claims, and the broad metric uses it (`lib/harness_forge_web/live/portal_live.ex:880,980`). The Company component separately loads `Company.list_plans/0` and renders each status (`company_panel.ex:362–374`; `company/plan.ex:228–245`). Thus the tile understates required work.

**Decision.** Replace that one broad tile with a compact two-row review summary: “Claims to review: N” links to Evidence; “Plans to review: M” leads to Company step 6, “Make a separate plan decision.” Count only records whose DTO has `status == "proposed"` and `superseded == false`. The latter matches the claim action guard (`portal_live.ex:1046`) and plan approval guard (`company.ex:330–334`). Keep the two counts separate; a claim decision never decides a plan. If either list cannot load, display “Unavailable” for that count and the existing storage error, never a false zero. Refresh both after a Company mutation through the existing `{:company_changed, _}` parent reload (`portal_live.ex:367`; `company_panel.ex:348`) and after claim events. A parent `Company.list_plans/0` read is the smallest direct implementation; no new database state or aggregate authority is needed. The component can retain its current plan read until a later consolidation.

**Acceptance tests.** Extend `test/harness_forge_web/company_builder_test.exs` and `portal_test.exs` with LiveView assertions: empty workspace shows 0/0; one active proposed claim alone shows 1/0; one proposed plan with no proposed claims shows 0/1; both show 1/1. Approving or rejecting each exact revision changes only its respective row after render. A proposed successor excludes its superseded predecessor from the count; rejected and approved predecessors do not reappear. A stale approved claim remains outside the review count and retains its separate stale warning. Simulate a plan-list read failure to assert “Unavailable” rather than 0. Assert the plan link reaches step 6 with a keyboard-reachable control and retains the exact revision decision form.

**Risk.** An extra plan query on every parent reload adds cost, and concurrent updates can briefly show a stale snapshot. Use the same reload cycle as other portal data and keep exact-revision checks in `Company.decide_plan/2`; the summary is navigation, not permission or build readiness.

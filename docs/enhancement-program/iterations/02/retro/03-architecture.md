Ranked missing capabilities:

1. **Complete policy traceability, including goal origins and declared conflicts.** [Evidence.create_claim](../../../../../lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex#L288) requires citations; [Decisions](../../../../../lib/harness_forge/decisions.ex#L7) validates them again; [Harnesses](../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex#L185) emits every rule with `goal_id: nil`. Although the harness schema permits goal references, selecting a goal supplies future intent, not approved policy. `records-v1alpha1.json` declares `rationale_kind`/`conflict_ids`, but persisted claims and admission have neither conflict-resolution records nor goal-origin basis. **Acceptance:** approve an exact goal-backed rule without fabricated historical citations; bind its basis/hash/decision through plan and export. Persist operator-declared conflict pairs and exact resolution decisions; unresolved or stale resolutions block approval/build/export. Reject forged, superseded and cross-scope references. Do not claim automatic semantic conflict detection.

2. **Qualified execution and company outcome evidence.** [Registry](../../../../../lib/harness_forge/adapters/registry.ex#L15) provides Codex 0.156.0/Claude Code 2.1.311 previews with unavailable managed backends. [Evaluations](../../../../../lib/harness_forge/evaluations.ex#L1) and release admission correctly separate integrity/smoke from qualified tasks. **Acceptance:** a consented real-model company pilot records comparable baseline/candidate tasks, exact runtime/model/environment identities, protected checks, budgets and verified cleanup. Any new managed backend must pass isolation/cancellation/forgery tests; unavailable routes remain blocked. Synthetic passes cannot satisfy this outcome criterion.

3. **Portable authenticated company authority.** Exported v1alpha1 provenance lacks the local company-plan approval binding; its digest proves consistency. **Acceptance:** a clean importer verifies signer trust, exact claim/goal/plan/package bindings and freshness/revocation policy, refusing tampering and unknown authority. Preserve historical artifact verification and restore quarantine through explicit versioning ([contract](../../../../company-builder-contract.md)).

**Iteration 03 functionality recommendation / ADR:** Context: policy authorship is incomplete. Decision: deliver item 1 through versioned domain/schema, admission/compiler, then UI units. Consequences: migration, privacy purge/restore and immutable compatibility need regression evidence. Alternatives: qualification-first or portable-authority-first remain valid priorities from the prior handoff; they leave policy expressiveness incomplete. This recommendation preserves that disagreement and uses no vote count.

## Ranked next gaps

1. **Intent completeness and explicit conflict decisions.** User: company engineering owner. Job: introduce desired policy without inventing historical evidence or silently combining incompatible rules. [Evidence.create_claim](../../../../../lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex) requires citations; [the builder](../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex) emits `goal_id: nil`. Goals/milestones cannot supply approved normative authority. Success: every emitted rule traces to its approved basis; zero unresolved declared conflicts compile. Falsifier: an approved intended rule is lost or contradictory declared rules compile. **AC:** Given approved goal-backed guidance and exact conflict resolution, when building, then only resolved rules compile. Given an unresolved declared conflict, when building, then refuse. Given changed goal acceptance, when rebuilding, then require renewed exact approval.

2. **Representative outcome qualification.** User: engineering team. Job: establish that its harness improves its actual tasks within enforced limits. Success: meet a predeclared company-task threshold against the same-task baseline, with zero false qualification. Falsifier: unsafe false pass or no measured benefit. **AC:** Given a supported runtime/model and approved suite, when evaluated, then bind results to exact identities and cleanup evidence. Given a failing grade, when promoting, then refuse. Given changed package/model/suite identity, when reusing results, then require reevaluation. The synthetic `synthetic-go-v1` path cannot establish this outcome.

3. **Portable approval authority.** User: receiving repository maintainer. Job: verify what the company approved after export. The [contract](../../../../company-builder-contract.md) explicitly retains company-plan authority locally. Success: all transferred rules/plan revisions are independently verifiable; no invalid receipt is accepted. Falsifier: accepted altered authority. **AC:** Given a valid authenticated receipt, when imported, then verify exact lineage. Given tampering, when imported, then reject. Given restored/quarantined authority, when used, then require fresh approval.

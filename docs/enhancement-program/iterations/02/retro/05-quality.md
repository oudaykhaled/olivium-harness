Ranked remaining reliability gaps:

1. **Company outcome qualification.** The fixed synthetic suite cannot establish fitness for a company's work. Acceptance: an authorized representative workload with at least three predefined must-pass tasks, five trials each, pinned artifact/model/runtime/suite/environment identities, protected grading, observed usage and complete cleanup; publish every result and a comparable baseline. A failed, stale or incomplete run must not qualify a release. Model inventory and live connector credentials/scopes remain unverified; managed Codex/Claude execution remains unavailable.
2. **Complete policy authority and portable provenance.** The [contract](../../../../company-builder-contract.md) explicitly defers goal-origin decisions, conflict resolution and portable composition approval. Acceptance: exact goal-basis revisions and explicit conflict decisions determine admission; a versioned exported proof lets a trusted clean importer verify actor authority, scope and exact approved composition, rejecting tampering/unresolved conflict while preserving legacy compatibility. Current local bindings cannot provide that guarantee.
3. **Sustained operational assurance.** Acceptance: fix both Dialyzer findings without suppression; publish measured coverage with a regression gate, 30-day first-attempt/retry records against a proposed ≤1% flake budget, and p50/p95/p99 under a declared workload with committed budgets. Keep short runtime paths within the known 104-byte socket constraint. No zero-flake or performance claim is currently justified.

Report blockers, identity mismatches, missing observations and cleanup state; test blocked/unqualified/stale/qualified transitions without granting authority. This provides immediate value without external prerequisites; an authorized real-model pilot then supplies stronger outcome evidence. Goal-policy and portable-approval priorities remain separate, unresolved alternatives.

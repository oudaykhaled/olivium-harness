# Iteration 11 UI/UX retrospective

**Was this tool really helpful and reliable to build a complete harness for a company? What is missing to make it reliable?**

HarnessForge helped demonstrate a deliberate, recoverable synthetic company workflow. Complete-company usefulness and novice independence remain unproven. The frozen verification describes four historical documents plus a goal, rejection and human correction, separate claim/plan approvals, two builds and corrected guidance in eight actual exported files. Scripted provider responses establish mechanics; the accepted owner report establishes neither truthful work nor harness quality. A deliberately false observation was also structurally accepted and visibly qualified as unverified. [V]

The assessment UI improves reliability through explicit stages, blank initial outcomes, field labels, linked error summaries, immutable receipts and distinct captured judgment/current applicability. Package and receipt identities have disclosures, although repeated hashes and mandatory mapping controls still compete with task meaning. Frozen Assessment renders presentation; CompanyAssessmentState owns drafts and transitions; PortalLive owns navigation, refresh and reset generations. Dirty departure offers keep, discard or strictly validated save. Failed saves retain the editor. Unsaved text survives the demonstrated source-tab return, while hard reload preserves committed records only. Reset/revocation protections are supported by source and reported root tests; I did not operate them. Stored-source links warn that destinations may change; exact excerpts remain separate evidence. [S; E02]

My image inspection covers two actual Chrome QA03 captures: a sampled fresh blank result and reloaded receipt. The saved/reopened QA03 DOM texts match byte-for-byte. The saved main image is explicitly **IAB**, following interrupted Chrome screenshot saving. The earlier eight-case 320/390 CSS-width ×100/200%-zoom matrix remains native02 historical evidence. Its metrics report contained scroll widths and sampled visible keyboard focus; I inspected the final 320/200 rubric/results photographs and focus metadata. Wrong-width and cropped earlier attempts are excluded. This supports bounded reflow/focus observations, with comprehensive keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, accessibility and novice task success still unproven. Canonical03's 777 passes/15 existing Docker skips and two preexisting Dialyzer diagnostics cannot establish those outcomes. [V; E02; E03; M]

Three recommendations should inform fresh planning:

1. **Make corpus scope and coverage understandable.** Replace the primary manual 2–4-document checklist with authorised corpus plus goal selection, paginated metadata, resumable progress, exclusions, stale/blocked items and explicit missing coverage. For hundreds/thousands/millions of files, show bounded relevant evidence and contradictory evidence with dates, policy/discussion status and surrounding context. Offer abstention when evidence is insufficient. Retrieval relevance must never look like company policy authority. [P; R]
2. **Make review and recovery readable.** Preserve deliberate edit/reject/approve actions and separate plan approval. Put supporting/conflicting excerpts beside recommendations with exact revision/hash/UTF-8 spans. Keep identity detail expandable, and provide understandable mapping summaries and explicit “same obligation/new meaning” successor actions without weakening stable-ID invariants. Retain WEB-11-01: fallback successor notice is inside a disclosure whose open condition excludes notice/predecessor; the focus helper skips hidden content. Keep guidance visible and focusable. WEB-11-02 manual mapping debt and CODE-11-02 unmeasured lineage latency remain. [S; E02; V]
3. **Validate one connected outcome at realistic scale.** Carry corpus→retrieval→human review→corrected actual export→representative evaluation through the next UI and following functionality planning. Measure reviewer errors, critical evidence misses, citation correctness, completion/recovery and time against a baseline. Reuse pinned Graphiti0.31.2@d72407dcedac911e1be9fc15b55e8c05a3efcc36/Jev extraction/runtime/hybrid through a bounded Python adapter, preserving Elixir/SQLite/FTS5 authority, nonempty scope, consent, stage metering and transitive dependencies. Show revoked derivatives blocked through failed rebuild/purge. The separate 51-test offline spike qualifies neither installed integration nor million-file/model quality. [R]

My dissent: more successful screens and “Accepted” receipts cannot answer whether a company received a complete, useful harness. Preserve the honest qualification and require representative evidence of understanding, correction and exported-task value. These are priorities for scope selection, not a selected next iteration.

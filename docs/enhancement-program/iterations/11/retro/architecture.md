My top three recommendations are:

1. **Deliver the company-corpus seam using the selected upstream engine.** Reuse Graphiti 0.31.2 at `d72407dcedac911e1be9fc15b55e8c05a3efcc36`, including real JevClient/runtime/extraction/hybrid retrieval, through a bounded Python adapter. Elixir and revisioned SQLite/FTS5 retain evidence, decisions and export authority. Add resumable capture, relational membership, metadata pagination and lazy passage hydration beyond the current manual 2–4-document journey. Avoid another graph/RAG engine or standalone vector platform. Pin and qualify the backend separately (`SCALABILITY_REUSE.md`; `REUSE-DECISION.md`).
2. **Carry authority through every derivative and dispatch.** Require server-derived nonempty groups, exact revision/hash/UTF-8 spans, parent context, dates/status, contradiction and abstention. Bind transitive prompt dependencies, including summaries and uncited inputs, to retrieval origins. Meter independently consented model stages and retries. Tombstone before removal and keep serving blocked through failed rebuilds; upstream restoration of an old summary cannot satisfy purge. Close selective mounted-view invalidation with late-event refusal while preserving unrelated drafts.
3. **Measure reliability at the actual bottlenecks.** Benchmark FTS5 and Graphiti on identical owner-reviewed supporting/conflicting evidence, citation correctness, missed critical obligations and representative-task outcomes. Measure ingestion recovery, concurrency, budgets and cap-sized lineage latency. `list_rubrics` repeatedly validates ancestry and successors under the privacy fence, yielding quadratic family work despite the 100-row cap; CODE-11-02 has no latency evidence. Prefer transaction-local validated graph reuse if measurements justify it, preserving fail-closed reads.

My dissent: more immutable receipts cannot compensate for omitted obligations, incomplete plan coherence or untruthful observations. The separate 51-test offline mock spike is research, not installed Graphiti, real-backend, semantic-quality or million-file proof.

Potential next UI priorities are corpus/goal selection, visible coverage/conflicts, successor recovery and simpler deliberate review. Following functionality should connect bounded retrieval through edit/reject/approve to corrected actual export and representative evaluation. These are planning inputs, not a selected future scope.

# Representative task and owner rubric

**User/job.** A company owner must judge whether an approved package helps representative work and covers declared mandatory obligations. Success means accepted task results for every obligation with zero critical violations; one accepted omission falsifies the hypothesis. Approved claims, goals and plan snapshots already reach build inputs (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:234-262,345-360`), but milestones may have no goal IDs and validation does not prove completeness (`lib/harness_forge/company/validation.ex:37-55,79-94`). Evaluation has only fixed `synthetic-go-v1` and trusted runs (`lib/harness_forge/evaluations.ex:1-16,82-95,790-802`; `lib/harness_forge/evaluation/run.ex:1-11`).

**Recommend:** after exact plan approval, let the owner define one task and immutable rubric traceable to approved guidance and selected goals, record observed evidence for each obligation, flag critical violations, and accept/reject against artifact digest, plan revision, goal hashes and rubric revision. Show rationale, conflicts, unknown coverage and unsupported runtime controls; do not claim all obligations were discovered. Draft/interrupted work stays incomplete; successor revisions require reassessment while preserving history. Store owner assertions separately from `Evaluation.Run`, release eligibility and `runtime_qualified`. Reuse privacy-fenced transactional audit (`company.ex:110-135,417-420`). Add the table to purge, backup/restore, immutable-history and quarantine inventories; restored acceptance loses authority (`privacy/snapshot.ex:7-8,55-86,286-350`). Bound record sizes and history.

**Acceptance.** Given an exact eligible package and mapped rubric, when every result has evidence and no critical violation, then acceptance and provenance persist. Given missing results, a critical violation, stale/revoked package, foreign workspace or forged revision, when accepting, then refuse without a trusted run. Given interruption, purge, restore or successor, when reopening history, then label retained bytes accurately, remove affected authority and require reassessment. Native synthetic portal/API checks cover review→export→task and privacy mutations; they establish mechanics, not semantic/runtime qualification.

**Alternative:** mounted-view invalidation after supported domain-source purge (`iterations/10/retro/03-security.md`) closes stale disclosure with less review, but leaves outcomes unobserved. Ownership: Company author, portal author, root for migration/privacy and corrections.

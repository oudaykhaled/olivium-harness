# 09 — Preserve deliberate decisions through the corpus handoff

**Recommendation.** Make one guided review continuation across the existing Corpus → Company → Evidence → Company Plan surfaces. Keep each decision as an explicit action on its displayed immutable revision. Show the current step and a return path at each transition; do not combine claim and plan approval into one control.

**Direct source observations.** Corpus shows original findings, uncertainty, goal rationale, human resolution and a versioned recommendation, then offers “Continue exact recommendation review” (`components/company/corpus_review.ex:435–510`). Its handler rechecks the claim ID/hash through `CorpusReview.recommendation` before redirecting to `/?company_review=...` (`live/corpus_review_live.ex:225–245`). Portal validates the revision and origin, and Company selects the exact claim (`live/portal_live.ex:81–96,1365–1399`; `components/company_panel.ex:191–219`). Company editing saves an **unapproved successor** with the original citations and report, and blocks leaving with unsaved edits (`company/analysis.ex:52–79,129–173`; `components/company_panel.ex:432–444,508–545`). The Evidence tab presents separate Reject/Approve actions for the exact claim revision (`live/portal_live.ex:2203–2314`). The plan form selects approved claim ID/hash pairs (`components/company/plan.ex:150–164,686–732`); its review has another approve/reject form with the plan ID/hash, and the handler calls `Company.decide_plan` (`components/company/plan.ex:573–668`; `components/company_panel.ex:700–710`). Build checks the selected approved plan revision (`components/company_panel.ex:1088–1113`). This is a strong authority boundary.

**Root-reported execution evidence.** Iteration 13’s handoff records a synthetic browser journey through human resolution, edited successor claim approval, separate plan approval and eight exported files. Static integrity passed; task evaluation was blocked by `model_not_selected`. Those receipts do not establish novice comprehension or real company usefulness.

**Smallest UI slice.** Add a compact, state-derived progress/next-action panel that follows the versioned recommendation into Company review, Evidence decision, exact plan proposal/decision, and build/export/evaluation. Show the exact claim and plan revision beside each decision, their different effects, and a clear “return to current recommendation” link. Keep the original report, supporting/contrary excerpts, goal rationale, uncertainty and human resolution adjacent to the claim decision; disclose hashes after the human-readable source labels. For a rejected claim, show its exclusion from plan choices and the route to propose a successor. Never auto-select or auto-approve a successor or plan.

**Acceptance and seam risks.** On a public fixture, verify keyboard navigation, an edited successor remaining proposed until its exact approval, rejection excluded from plan selection, changed/revoked origin refusing stale handoffs, unsaved edits retained or explicitly discarded, and distinct claim/plan decisions before actual export. Show static integrity and blocked task evaluation as different outcomes. Avoid copying authoritative state into UI-only flags: derive steps from existing claim, plan, currentness and preflight reads. A redirect must revalidate ID/hash and clear stale selected evidence. Preserve the v2 corpus claim’s origin and manifest when crossing legacy Company/Evidence components; do not route it through the old 2–4-source input.

**Defer to 15.** Semantic relevance/abstention, real-model task benefit, portable approval receipts and scale qualification need functional evidence. The proposed four-of-five novice target in retros is advice, not an adopted gate; a packaged public-fixture journey and independent comprehension review are feasible 14 checks, while real novice usability stays unqualified without participants.

# Proposal 04 — declared conflict admission

**Recommendation (proposed ADR).** Let an owner declare that two exact approved claim revisions conflict within a project, then require an approved successor plan to choose one governing revision before build/export. Every *declared* in-scope conflict gets an explicit, current decision. This cannot discover contradictions or prove obligation coverage.

**Context.** Claim decisions pin immutable revisions (`lib/harness_forge/decisions.ex:7-35`); `Company.resolve/1` checks selected approved claims, goals and source scope, but no pairwise conflict or coverage (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:234-261`). Plan approval and build recheck its snapshot (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:110-169,328-346`). Direct source-only builds bypass plans (`lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:93-117`), while export rechecks eligibility (`lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:122-170`). The prior conflict-first argument remains substantive (`docs/enhancement-program/iterations/05/retro/03-architecture.md`); 06's five retrospectives favor route identity first, without resolving policy coherence (`docs/enhancement-program/iterations/06/handoff-to-07.md`).

**Decision and consequences.** Store a workspace/project-scoped immutable declaration keyed by both claim IDs and revision hashes. Add an optional, hash-pinned plan resolution naming the governing member; only that member enters compiled rules. Reuse separate plan decision, transaction/privacy fence and prepublication recheck. Refuse direct builds and export eligibility when an active declaration involves their claims. Existing plans retain their bytes; a new relevant declaration makes them ineligible until reviewed. Supersession, withdrawal, scope change, purge or restore invalidates affected authority. This adds schema, privacy and compatibility work.

**Alternatives.** A validated task-route snapshot (`lib/harness_forge/execution/coding.ex:28-38,427-448`) is smaller and fixes a source-derived identity ambiguity before qualification; no route-drift exploit has been observed. Operator prose costs less but cannot enforce admission. I favor conflict admission for the direct company-policy benefit if one iteration can carry its full lifecycle; otherwise choose the route unit, never a partial conflict gate.

**Confirmation.** Positive: declared pair plus separately approved exact winner plan builds/exports only the winner. Negative: unresolved, forged, foreign-scope or direct-build attempt publishes no new bytes. Edge: changed claim, plan, declaration or restored workspace refuses stale resolution; unrelated plans and legacy hashes remain valid. Domain owner: Company/validation/records and migration; root integrates shared migration/privacy; QA owns focused admission, export, purge/restore and legacy-byte checks. No runtime verification was performed for this proposal.

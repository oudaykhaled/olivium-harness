# Proposal 06 — Question-aware bounded evidence packing

**Problem and hypothesis.** The public nine-content-term question selected inventory, incident and exception passages but missed `policy.md`; the focused question selected all four. `Retrieval.tokens/1` drops middle terms after eight, while the independent obligations and generic follow-up lanes can occupy the fixed 24-passage, 32 KiB packet. The observed miss demonstrates a query loss; competition from those lanes is a plausible additional risk, not a proven cause of this miss. A versioned, question-aware candidate/packing policy should improve indispensable policy and contrary recall without flooding synthesis with unrelated goal material.

**Small WEB/backend slice.** In existing `Company.Retrieval`, retain every distinct content term from the accepted 256-character question in the versioned query plan, or fail visibly if the declared FTS expression cap cannot represent it. First trial a bounded full-term OR expression in the existing five SQLite FTS5 lanes; do not enlarge candidate/prompt/dispatch limits. For packing, rank checked candidates within the current lane reservations using lexical question overlap, lane rank and source diversity. Give question-grounded support and contrary evidence first consideration; keep goal-only obligations and generic follow-up available but mark their question overlap and inclusion reason. Never treat lexical overlap or a provisional lane label as policy authority. If the packet cannot fit potentially decisive candidates, expose truncation/insufficiency and abstain from synthesis under the existing readiness contract. Version the plan/packing envelope and preserve the complete selected and prompt-influencing dependency lineage. `Passages`, `OriginV2`, `AnalysisV2`, currentness/consent/budget/cancel gates, claim approval, separate plan approval, compiler/export and evaluations remain the authority seams. Pinned Graphiti 0.31.2/native Jev remains the existing hybrid route.

**Acceptance.** Root authors public development corpora/questions independently of protected holdouts: the retained long/focused case, reordered middle policy term, paraphrase, later exception/incident, distractor-heavy and no-answer cases. Compare frozen baseline and candidate at identical caps; record exact source/revision expected support and contrary recall, missed obligations, irrelevant selected bytes, packet omissions, abstention, p95 and resource use on declared hardware. An answerable case cannot pass by abstaining. Inspect exact parent context and document status; require human correction of an unsupported or incomplete proposal, rejection of a canary, exact claim and **separate** exact plan approval, then verify corrected rule and scope in actual export bytes. A representative task remains `BLOCKED model_not_selected/not_dispatched` unless an authorised configured model exists; static integrity is distinct.

**Alternatives and decision.** A full-term OR is the smallest trial, but common terms may dilute BM25 top-20 ranking. Bounded multi-query decomposition can protect rare middle terms, at extra FTS searches/fusion complexity; use it only if the public comparison shows the simpler trial misses critical evidence. Raising `top-k` or adding another retrieval framework increases cost/irrelevance without addressing query omission. No real-company quality or physical scale claim follows from these tests.

# Bounded whole-question FTS for reviewed company guidance

**Observed defect.** Public browser evidence shows the nine-content-term question selected three passages and omitted `policy.md`; a shorter question selected four including the two-reviewer policy. The frozen `Retrieval.tokens/1` retains the first four and last four unique content terms, dropping middle “policy” (`retrieval.ex:112–146`). This is one reproducible miss, not a measured recall rate. A user who never rewrites the question may approve guidance without the general rule.

**Proposal to test, not a preselected release.** Keep the same five FTS5 lanes, scope SQL, 20-per-lane limit, 100-candidate ceiling, 24-passage/32 KiB packing and current authority checks. In `Company.Retrieval.query_plan/2`, generate the lexical expressions from **all unique content terms in the already limited 256-character/1,024-byte question**, with a versioned plan and explicit term-count diagnostic. Do not silently discard terms. If worst-case term count or FTS execution cannot meet a declared latency budget, fail or mark search incomplete visibly; never call a partial search “ready.” Keep the goal, contrary, chronology and follow-up lanes and context-diverse packing. Compare the existing baseline with this candidate before selection. Full-term OR is the smallest local change and uses no new engine, but frequent generic terms can dilute BM25 and push policy below the top 20. Five 30-second lane timeouts can make worst-case latency unacceptable; measure p50/p95 and timeout incidence on declared hardware rather than assume the current bounds suffice.

**Alternative.** Two bounded subqueries (first/middle/last coverage) can recover rare terms when full OR ranking dilutes them, but require additional FTS executions or lane fusion and a new provenance representation. Packing-only changes cannot retrieve a passage absent from candidates. A manually focused question is an operator workaround, not dependable whole-question behavior. Prefer full-term treatment only if public development cases show critical/contrary recall without unacceptable irrelevant inclusion or latency; otherwise compare bounded decomposition under the same total search/candidate/evidence budgets.

**Connected acceptance.** Independently author public company cases with policy in each question position, reordered/paraphrased terms, applicable emergency exception, historical failure, distractors and unanswerable cases. Record expected-source critical/contrary misses, selected-versus-candidate losses, context truncation, citation support, abstention and latency against baseline. The original long question must surface policy plus exception/failure within declared caps; answerable cases cannot all abstain. Use checked `Passages`, versioned `OriginV2`, `AnalysisV2` and the existing web review to expose uncertainty and let the human correct/reject a proposal. Exact claim approval and **separate** exact plan approval must lead to corrected actual export bytes; rejected guidance must be absent. Preserve consent, generation/revocation fences, transitive dependencies, dispatch budgets/cancellation and immutable output. Existing `Evaluations` reports task `BLOCKED` until an authorised configured model can run a paired representative comparison. Static integrity and scripted synthesis establish mechanics only.

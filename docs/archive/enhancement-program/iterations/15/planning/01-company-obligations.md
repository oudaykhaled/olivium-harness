# Company obligations through a reviewed harness

**Proposal (functionality, web plus supporting Elixir):** Preserve indispensable policy and contrary evidence when a company owner asks a natural, multi-part question. The public iteration-14 browser record shows a nine-content-term question selecting three passages and omitting `policy.md`; a focused question selected four, including that general two-reviewer policy. In the curated `Company.Retrieval` snapshot, `tokens/1` keeps the first four and last four content terms when there are more than eight, dropping the middle “policy” term. This is one demonstrated failure, not a recall-rate estimate; packing and ranking may also contribute.

**Smallest decision to test:** Compare bounded full-question terms, bounded multi-query decomposition, and a packing adjustment against the unchanged FTS5 baseline. Full terms preserve the question cheaply but may dilute ranking; multiple queries protect distinct concepts but add searches and fusion complexity; packing can rescue an available candidate but cannot recover a term never searched. Select only after independently authored **public development** cases measure misses, irrelevant inclusion, selected-context bytes and latency under declared caps. Retain the original long/short pair as a regression case; vary decisive-term position and wording, later emergency exceptions, failed rollback rehearsals, distractors and unanswerable questions. Do not use protected holdouts or expected values.

**Existing seams:** `Retrieval.query_plan/2` and `packing_plan/1` already generate five bounded FTS5 lanes, candidate diagnostics and a versioned run; `Passages` checks exact source/revision/hash/UTF-8 spans; `OriginV2` seals selected and transitive dependencies. `AnalysisV2` includes the saved goal, provisional relations and explicit uncertainty in the prompt. Continue through the current browser review, human correction or rejection, exact claim decision, **separate** exact plan decision, compiler/export and `Evaluations`. Keep Graphiti 0.31.2 native Jev integration available under its current authority; no new engine or model installation.

**Connected acceptance:** On the same authorised synthetic corpus and saved future goal, answerable long, short and reordered questions expose the applicable policy, exception and failure evidence within the selected prompt budget; the reviewed recommendation distinguishes ordinary changes, emergency scope, abandoned discussion and unknown currentness. A reviewer can correct it, reject an unsupported canary, approve the exact successor claim and separately approve the exact plan. Inspect actual exported file bytes for the corrected scoped rule and check, goal link and provenance; rejected and superseded guidance stays absent. Record indispensable and contrary source misses, citation support, abstention, omissions and latency. Unanswerable cases abstain; all-abstention fails answerable cases. Revoked or changed inputs invalidate downstream authority. Static integrity can pass while representative task assessment remains `BLOCKED model_not_selected` until an authorised configured model supports paired observations.

**Limit:** The scripted four-file journey proves workflow mechanics, not real-company semantics, complete corpus coverage, million-file scale or task benefit. A check saying only “a reviewer” remains weaker than the two-reviewer ordinary rule; assess this as an obligation-coverage risk rather than a proven downstream violation.

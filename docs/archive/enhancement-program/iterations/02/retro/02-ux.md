# Iteration 02 retrospective — UX and comprehension

**Question:** “Was this tool really helpfull and reliable to build a complete harness for a company? and what is missing to make it reliable?”

**Verdict:** Helpful for controlled local assembly; complete-company reliability remains unproven. HarnessForge now gives an operator a meaningful route from saved company context to an explicitly reviewed plan and immutable exported package. That is useful progress. It does not establish that the resulting harness covers a company's policies, works with its real integrations, or improves real work.

The Company guide reduces simultaneous form exposure while preserving direct access and drafts. Saved prerequisites, separate claim/plan queues and selected-plan preflight prevent several misleading states. An approved plan can correctly remain approved while changed context blocks building; a proposed successor does not invalidate an eligible predecessor. These distinctions are supported by [Guide.project/1 and next_action/5](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge_web/components/company/guide.ex), the [native lifecycle records](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/02/browser/browser-manifest.json), and the independent reviews.

I inspected actual calibrated **1463×672 and 390×844 action frames**, rather than the downsampled full captures. The focused recovery button is legible and 44px high; its text wraps without clipping. The initial desktop frame ends before the action, while the narrow guide places substantial copy, status cards and revision details ahead of it. Repeated stale-context paragraphs add reading burden. This is an observed presentation issue, not measured novice failure. Recorded 320px geometry shows no measured main overflow; it does not establish true 200% zoom or accessibility conformance. See [viewport evidence](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/02/browser/guide-action-390.png) and [UX validation](../review/ux-validation.md).

The reliability evidence is substantial within scope: **417 unique passing tests** across canonical and Docker runs, plus native synthetic build/export and reset/queued-submit proof. Two unchanged Dialyzer diagnostics remain. Neither those tests nor successful export prove real-model company qualification. Native reset evidence demonstrates rejection, restored saved controls and unchanged company/audit data; hidden nonce rotation was not directly observed. [QA evidence](../review/regression-validation.md)

## Ranked gaps and acceptance

1. **Qualification readiness and demonstrated company fit — iteration 03 FUNCTIONALITY priority.** Reuse existing evaluation/release gates to return readiness for one exact package, with a specific blocker and next action. Acceptance: integrity, current eligibility, runtime/model availability, task outcome and release qualification remain distinct; digest, plan, runtime/model/suite identities and cleanup evidence stay linked. Missing models, unsupported managed runtimes, stale inputs and the known short socket-path constraint produce actionable blocked states. Exercise passed, failed, cancelled and unavailable cases; require an authorized representative company pilot before claiming company fitness. Existing synthetic container tests must not be relabeled real inference.

2. **Trustworthy recovery across the Company→Packages handoff — next UI priority.** A missing export parent currently produces provider-import advice; successful retry retains that error. Duplicate repository identity receives a broad checklist mentioning unrelated claims/milestones. Acceptance: each known refusal identifies its actual cause and safe correction, retains valid input and focuses the owning summary; successful retry clears the superseded error and announces one unambiguous result. Test missing/existing parent, existing target, duplicate identity and retry sequences. [Observed export result](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/02/browser/export-success-dom.txt); [source](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge_web/live/portal_live.ex).

3. **Comprehension and accessible completion.** Put the current action before detailed progress/provenance, disclose repeated explanations and preserve exact identity access. Acceptance: record unassisted representative users completing empty→review→export and recovering a stale plan; ask them to distinguish approval, buildability and qualification. Record assistance/errors, then resolve observed blockers. Verify keyboard/screen-reader journeys, focus, contrast and real 200% zoom. These are proposed checks; no user-study metrics or certification exist yet.

Goal-origin policy/conflict resolution and portable approval authority remain separate gaps. I prioritize qualification readiness ahead of them; that ordering is a judgment, not consensus or proof they are solved.

Basis: all 19 frozen inputs, all 56 browser artifact hashes and eight final source hashes matched. No peer retrospective was read, runtime launched, or ongoing main upgrade assumed verified.

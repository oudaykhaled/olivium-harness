# Authority and exact-claim review navigation

**Recommendation.** Give Company a prerequisite summary and one link to the existing review control. Keep claim decisions in Evidence and plan decisions in Company. Approval authority and eligibility remain server-side.

**Code evidence.** The workspace metric labels `proposed(@claims)` “AWAITING A DECISION,” omitting plans, while the four-stage banner always marks “Connect sources” complete (`portal_live.ex:979-988`). Evidence already displays exact claim status, revision hash, citation snippet/byte range and revision-bound approve/reject controls (`portal_live.ex:1027-1071`). Company step 4 links to Evidence; step 6 separately reviews immutable plans (`company/plan.ex:44-61,225-249`). `Evidence.approved_claims/0` excludes superseded claims and invalid citations; `Company.approved_claim/1` rechecks exact hash, approval and citations, then `claim_sources/2` enforces selected source membership and current active revision (`evidence.ex:344-361`; `company.ex:267-295`).

**Bounded design.** Derive separate “claims awaiting review” and “plans awaiting review” counts from current saved records; exclude superseded proposals from actionable counts. Use a Company prerequisite row for captured active source, approved eligible claim in the selected project’s source scope, and exact selected plan decision. Its action opens Evidence at the relevant claim, or Company at the plan review, with a stable target and keyboard focus. A summary may show IDs, status and a short revision fingerprint; avoid citation snippets, source bodies, credentials or cross-workspace counts. Loading errors show “unavailable” rather than zero or complete. Never route an approval click from summary or infer approval from navigation.

**Acceptance.** Empty: no source-complete badge; link to source connection. Missing or proposed claim: link to source capture or exact Evidence review. Rejected claim: explain that a new proposal/revision is needed; do not relabel rejected as pending. Approved but stale/revoked source: show ineligible and link to evidence/source repair, never build-ready. Approved current claim outside project scope: show assignment blocker, not eligible. Proposed plan: separate plan count and exact-plan review link. Approved current plan: next action may lead to build; stale plan returns to re-proposal/review. Check every state with synthetic workspace data, keyboard navigation and narrow viewport.

**Security risk.** A cached UI summary can misstate authority after revocation or membership change. Recompute on reload and after decisions, and retain the existing server-side admission checks; the UI must never be the authorization gate.

**Independent product retrospective — iteration 01**

Was this tool really helpful and reliable to build a complete harness for a company? **Helpful for inspecting a deliberately assembled company harness; complete-company reliability remains unproven.** A platform engineer can now ask an MCP client which saved service matches a capability, whether an exact plan is buildable, and whether a package remains eligible. This reduces context switching and mistaken selection. It does not demonstrate that a company captured the right policies, resolved competing goals, or obtained better coding outcomes.

The evidence supports that bounded benefit. The [native client report](../review/client-validation.md) records official MCP SDK **1.29.0**, protocol **2025-11-25**, and **44 passing checks**, including proposed/rejected refusal and an approved predecessor surviving a proposed successor. All 30 database tables and 10 artifact files retained identical content. The [security review](../review/security-review.md) found no remaining actionable issue in the frozen surface after the no-store exception fix. The [regression report](../review/regression-validation.md) records 388 passes, zero failures, 15 existing skips, unmeasured coverage and two unchanged Dialyzer diagnostics. These are useful reliability signals, not customer outcome measurements. No real model, live connector or managed coding journey ran. [Package inspection](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/mcp/tools.ex:253) explicitly reports qualification unassessed and portable approval verification unavailable.

My ranked gaps are:

1. **Prove useful, qualified use.** User: a company engineering lead; job: trust a harness on representative work. Success metric: five predefined company tasks meet their acceptance checks with recorded review effort, against the same tasks without the harness. The value hypothesis fails if outcomes or review effort do not improve; safety fails on any unauthorized action. Acceptance: **Given** an approved eligible digest, **when** a qualified isolated pilot runs, **then** results identify the digest, model/runtime versions and each task outcome. **Given** unavailable isolation or model routing, **when** evaluation is requested, **then** it remains unavailable without host/provider fallback. **Given** source revocation between preparation and execution, **when** admission repeats, **then** the stale package cannot qualify. The current [adapter registry](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/adapters/registry.ex:15) marks every runtime unqualified.

2. **Represent deliberate company intent and conflicting instructions.** User: a policy owner; job: express a new operating goal without inventing source evidence. Success metric: every emitted rule has an inspectable origin and exact approval; no selected conflict is silently resolved. The hypothesis fails if operators still need to mislabel goals as sourced facts. Today [specification construction](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:184) creates rules from claims with `goal_id: nil`, while goals remain separate. Acceptance: **Given** an owned goal, **when** a goal-origin rule is proposed and separately approved, **then** its immutable origin and decision are inspectable. **Given** an unapproved rule, **when** building, **then** it is excluded or blocks the build explicitly. **Given** conflicting approved instructions, **when** planning, **then** both revisions remain visible until an explicit resolution. This is my recommended next functionality gap, before a broader pilot.

3. **Help operators finish the workflow.** User: a first-time local operator; job: find the next valid action. Success metric: four of five observed newcomers reach an approved synthetic package without coaching; failure falsifies the onboarding hypothesis. The [browser baseline](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/01/browser/company-dom.txt) exposes seven long setup sections, including downstream forms before prerequisites. For iteration 02, add a state-aware Company summary with one primary next action and expandable existing forms. Acceptance: **Given** missing context, **when** opening Company, **then** the next action targets the required control. **Given** unavailable evaluation, **when** viewing progress, **then** text explains the blocker without implying qualification. **Given** keyboard-only use at narrow width, **when** following that action, **then** focus reaches the control and drafts survive navigation.

Remaining doubts: no newcomer study establishes the suspected navigation burden; portable approval trust and live connector credential/scope gaps remain unresolved. I disagree with treating additional MCP tools as the immediate product priority: guided completion and intent fidelity currently offer clearer value. These are retrospective recommendations, not next-scope approval.

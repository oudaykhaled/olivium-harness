# 05 proposal: explicit goal-origin claim basis

**Context.** `Evidence.create_claim/1` and `approved_claims/0` require citations (`evidence.ex:288–385`); `Company.approved_claim/1` repeats citation validation (`company.ex:267–273`). Yet `Harnesses.specification/3` emits `goal_id: nil` (`harnesses.ex:185–195`). The v1alpha1 schema and `Contracts.semantic_errors/1` allow `evidence: []` with a supplied `goal_id`; `Compiler` emits that identity in provenance (`compiler.ex:76–84`). Plan snapshots pin goal content (`company.ex:245–260,370–371`) without granting rule authority. The 04 retrospectives leave company fitness unproven and 05 priority disputed. The 03 amendment keeps current OpenCode task admission unassessed.

**Decision (one functionality unit).** Add a nullable, immutable claim `basis` with exact shape `{version: 1, kind: "goal", goal_id: UUID, goal_content_hash: SHA-256}`. A goal-origin claim has `citations: []`; its revision hash includes the canonical basis. Existing `Decisions.decide/2` approves that exact revision; selecting a goal grants no authority. Resolve the goal in the current workspace and compare its content hash at proposal and decision. Plan approval, build and export additionally require it in selected `goal_ids`/snapshot. Render its v1alpha1 rule as `goal_id` plus empty evidence, without schema extension. Source-origin claims retain their old hash input, `goal_id: nil`, citation checks and exact artifact bytes. Reject unknown basis versions/kinds.

**Acceptance.** A goal-origin claim needs exact approval and an approved plan; build/export yield matching rule and goal provenance. Reject missing/foreign goal, wrong hash, absent plan selection, rejected/superseded claim, changed plan and restored approvals. Source-only golden spec, manifest, provenance and digest remain byte-identical; old artifact verification succeeds. Source purge leaves unrelated goal claims; workspace purge removes them. Restore quarantines decisions (`privacy/snapshot.ex:245`) and cannot reacquire authority silently. Check build/export paths and negative cases without claiming completeness or qualified delivery.

**Ownership/risk.** Evidence/Decisions plus migration own immutable claim shape/hash and validation; Company owns selected-goal admission; Harnesses owns rule mapping; root owns shared Contracts/schema integration and privacy review. Main risk is changing legacy canonical bytes or accepting an old approval after a basis mutation. Golden-byte regression and exact-revision/restore tests gate acceptance. Alternatives: implicit selected-goal authority is unsafe; a new interchange version adds migration cost without a needed field; declared-conflict and current-route contracts remain separate candidates.

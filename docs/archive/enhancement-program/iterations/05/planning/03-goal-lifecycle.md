# Goal-origin authority with explicit lifecycle

**One functionality benefit.** Let a company owner turn a selected future goal into an approved rule without fabricating a source citation. A goal is intent until an accountable actor approves an exact rule revision. Today `Goals.add_goal/1` records title, owner and acceptance; `Evidence.create_claim/1` and `Decisions.decide/2` require citations; `Harnesses.specification/3` always writes `goal_id: nil` ([Goals](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/goals.ex), [Evidence](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex), [Decisions](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/decisions.ex), [Harnesses](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex)). `Company.resolve/1` already pins selected goal hashes, but selection alone grants no rule authority ([Company](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/company.ex)).

**Contract.** Add a versioned `future_goal` claim basis carrying workspace-scoped goal ID and canonical hash of its title/owner/acceptance; citations must be empty. Keep legacy citation-backed claims and their revision hashes/bytes unchanged. Bind basis, rule text and goal hash into each *new* claim revision hash. Approval rechecks the exact goal and expected claim hash. A company plan explicitly selects the approved claim and its goal, pins both hashes, and emits `goal_id` only for that claim. Build, export and release admission revalidate the current basis through existing eligibility paths; old exported packages remain immutable records, never current authority. Do not infer a rule from goal text or revive a superseded claim.

**Lifecycle acceptance.** A fresh goal-backed claim can be approved, planned, built and exported with exact typed provenance. Empty/mixed bases, wrong-workspace or unselected goals, changed goal content, supersession and withdrawn approval fail closed. Source purge retains unrelated goal authority but invalidates any selected citation-backed dependencies; workspace purge removes the basis and decisions. Backup restore preserves historical bytes while clearing decisions and quarantining the workspace; no restored approval or release readiness appears until fresh approval and evaluation ([Purge](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/purge.ex), [Snapshot](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/snapshot.ex)). Test old backups and legacy artifact identity, plus approval/build/export/release transitions. Keep goal text inside the existing privacy boundary; audit identifiers/hashes only.

**Choice.** This is narrower than conflict resolution, which needs declaration semantics, and current task-route identity, which needs trusted execution provenance. The five [04 retrospectives](../handoff-to-05.md) leave these as competing gaps; neither is solved here. Representative company/model outcomes and portable authenticated approval remain unproven.

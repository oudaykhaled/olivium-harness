# 07 — One clear step after saving a goal

**Observed facts.** `PortalLive.handle_event("goal", ...)` saves and shows a generic flash, leaving the operator on Goals (`lib/harness_forge_web/live/portal_live.ex:420-422`). The Goals render lists saved cards without a route to the separate rule form (`:1856-1889`). Evidence has that form and explains that goal selection does not approve a rule (`:1446-1505`). The only nearby reverse path is Evidence’s “Save a goal” button (`:1454-1461`). Company already offers deliberate cross-tab navigation and focus through `{:company_navigate, tab}` (`:473-478`); its guide tells operators to review exact claims (`lib/harness_forge_web/components/company/guide.ex:158-163`). The iteration05 native save left BODY focused, though the next Tab reached Goal; no destination-focus or comprehension claim follows (`docs/enhancement-program/iterations/05/verification.md`).

**Proposal (hypothesis, not observed usability).** After a successful goal save, show a contextual, keyboard-operable **“Continue to Evidence to propose a rule”** action beside the saved goal result. Activating it opens Evidence and focuses the goal-rule proposal heading or form start via the existing focus mechanism. Keep the saved goal **unselected**: the operator chooses its current version and authors a separate rule. This closes a memory-dependent handoff without adding another wizard or implying approval. Scope: PortalLive’s goal result/navigation and focused web tests; reuse existing `company-focus` handling. No Company plan, approval, export, persistence or shared authority change.

**Acceptance.** Positive: saving a goal presents one clear next action; keyboard activation reaches Evidence’s proposal and the following Tab reaches the saved-goal select. Negative: failed save shows no success action; navigation alone creates no claim, selection, decision or plan. Edge: unavailable goal records show the existing unavailable explanation instead of an actionable proposal; remount, lock/revocation and privacy reset clear transient result/action; stale submission cannot resurrect it. Check at 320/390 CSS pixels and actual 200% zoom for visible, unclipped action and focus order. Human comprehension and screen-reader speech require separate evidence.

**Dissent retained.** Product, architecture and trust favored Packages export recovery; quality favored operation-owned notices. The stale export/direct-build feedback is real and remains a distinct candidate, not part of this unit. OpenCode current-task inspection remains command-free and Not assessed under accepted ADR03.

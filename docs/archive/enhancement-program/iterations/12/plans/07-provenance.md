# Planner 07 — provenance at the review decision

**User benefit.** After choosing an authorised corpus and saved goal, a reviewer inspects bounded supporting or conflicting passages in parent context. Each card shows source link, exact revision/hash/UTF-8 span, parent excerpt, document status, effective/event and capture dates, and explicit “unknown” for absent fields. Relevance and graph confidence stay separate from policy authority. The first screen groups support, conflict, missing coverage and abstention; identity details expand on demand.

**Frozen observations.** Today the analysis form requires 2–4 checked sources and one goal and warns unchecked material is excluded (`lib/harness_forge_web/components/company/analysis.ex:195–285`). The report shows exact quote and byte range, then current title and capture time, while its other context/hashes sit in a separate disclosure (`:453–542`). `citation_metadata/3` only projects metadata after matching current scoped source, revision, hash and workspace (`:577–610`); its fallback retains the original pin. The source metadata tests cover stale revisions, same-hash foreign identity, escaped text and unsafe URLs (`test/harness_forge_web/company_analysis_metadata_test.exs:16–180`). Model validation checks UTF-8 boundaries and exact snippet bytes (`lib/harness_forge/models/company_analysis.ex:136–159`), but citation shape carries no parent passage or historical status (`:6–16`). Current analysis sends whole selected bodies and pins examined but uncited refs (`lib/harness_forge/company/analysis.ex:207–274`; `lib/harness_forge/evidence/origin.ex:24–44`). These are mechanics, not semantic truth or scale proof.

**Necessary seam and ownership.** Domain exposes a bounded, server-authorised passage projection keyed by immutable revision/hash/span and versioned retrieval origin: corpus/goal, query/index generation, examined cited/uncited passages and summaries, coverage and contrary dependencies. Parent boundaries, effective/event dates and status are captured or unknown, never inferred from capture order. Resolve lazily under nonempty server-derived workspace/project/group scope; deny removed or changed material before rendering. UI owner changes Company analysis and PortalLive to page cards, retain drafts, expose staleness and focus conflict/recovery notices. `Decisions.decide/2` requires exact revision hash and refuses successors (`lib/harness_forge/decisions.ex:7–18`); preserve separate claim/plan approval. Graphiti discovers evidence; Elixir/SQLite retain authority.

**Acceptance.** Positive: a larger synthetic corpus shows support and contrary passages with exact quotes, parent context and historical statuses; correction creates an unapproved successor, then explicit claim/plan approvals produce corrected actual target bytes and representative assessment. Negative: parent-negated quotes, missing dates/status, foreign scope or new uncited contradiction cannot appear as current approved guidance; unknown coverage stays visible. Recovery: change or tombstone while open blocks link/body and decision, retains a safe draft and offers fresh scoped retrieval; failed derivative rebuild stays blocked. Test DOM/focus, domain integration, exact export and stale-origin refusal. Estimate: 4–6 engineer days across domain/UI plus validation; passage capture may raise this. Quote-only cards are cheaper but miss reversal and chronology. A graph explorer adds navigation and authority ambiguity before quality is measured.

# 08 — Privacy-safe company evidence scope and recovery

**Bounded user benefit.** In the Company journey, let an operator choose one authorised corpus and one saved goal, then see a compact scope header and a paginated, bounded evidence preview. Each preview marks supporting, conflicting, unavailable or pending-removal evidence with source revision, date/status and a short exact passage. A privacy change immediately replaces affected previews and recommendations with a blocked state; unaffected draft edits remain available. The operator can resume a failed purge and, after fresh capture, re-run analysis and deliberately edit/reject/approve a new claim and plan before corrected actual export and representative assessment. This UI slice should make revocation legible during the company-scale journey, not offer a whole-corpus checklist.

**Frozen observations.** Current Company analysis event shape accepts at most five `source_ids` (`source-before/lib/harness_forge_web/components/company_panel.ex:697–701`). `PortalLive` handles purge/restore (`:523–536`), broadcasts `:company_cache_reset` only on successful privacy results (`:894–918`), and resets mounted Company/assessment state on that message (`:920–929,603–609`). Reload refreshes assessments but is tied to supported events (`:939–987`); a direct supported domain change can leave mounted content until reload, as the handoff records. The present two-view purge test proves successful source purge clears drafts and rejects old generations (`source-before/test/harness_forge_web/company_assessment_flow_test.exs:287–310`). A failed cleanup leaves a durable `prepared` operation and `Privacy.ready/0` refuses use (`source-before/lib/harness_forge/privacy/purge.ex:10–18,62–118`; `source-before/test/harness_forge/privacy_test.exs:166–194`). Restore revokes connections and removes approvals, model settings and jobs (`source-before/lib/harness_forge/privacy/snapshot.ex:288–340`). Upstream Graphiti turns empty groups into unfiltered search (`sources/graphiti/graphiti_core/search/search.py:100–110,155–156`); `remove_episode` can retain an old summary after rebuild failure (`graphiti.py:2215–2251`).

**Smallest working seam and ownership.** Root owns shared migration/router/Portal integration. An evidence-domain author supplies server-derived nonempty workspace/project group membership, paginated metadata and exact passage hydration, and rejects retrieval **before** Graphiti/FTS5 and every model dispatch if scope, revision, consent or privacy fence is invalid. A privacy author records source/revision tombstones before graph removal, invalidates versioned retrieval origins and all transitive prompt dependencies including uncited summaries, and keeps affected derivatives unservable until verified cleanup/rebuild; restore starts quarantined. A UI author changes CompanyPanel/Analysis presentation to show scope, coverage gaps, conflicting evidence and an explicit abstention state, with selective mounted invalidation and stale-event refusal. Elixir/revisioned SQLite remains authority; Graphiti 0.31.2 is used through the selected bounded Python adapter, never as access policy or approval authority. Keep stage-specific consent/budget checks, exact human claim/plan decisions and deterministic export.

**Acceptance and effort.** Positive: a larger public synthetic corpus plus goal yields a bounded page, visible contrary passage, exact revision/hash/UTF-8 span and a corrected approved export/assessment path. Negative: empty/foreign group, revoked source or stale mounted event yields no passage, dispatch or authority; unrelated draft remains. Recovery: inject summary-rebuild or file-cleanup failure, observe blocked UI and no serving, resume cleanup, re-capture and re-review without reviving old approval. Compare the same owner-reviewed questions against FTS5 for critical misses and citation correctness; scripted providers establish mechanics only. Estimate 5–8 engineering days plus independent browser/privacy tests. Alternative: reset all mounted Company state on every change, simpler but destroys unrelated edits and obscures which evidence was removed. I prefer selective invalidation with a fail-closed full reset when dependency identity cannot be resolved.

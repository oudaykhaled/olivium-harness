# 08 proposal: continue from a saved goal to its evidence rule

**Problem and benefit.** Saved goal cards end after owner, title and acceptance text (`lib/harness_forge_web/live/portal_live.ex:1952-1984`). The separate evidence form requires blank saved-goal selection (`:1542-1602`); the browser test confirms this (`test/harness_forge_web/goal_claims_test.exs:28-52`). Add “Propose a rule from this goal” to each card. Activation switches to Evidence and focuses the goal-rule heading; next Tab reaches the blank Saved goal select. The next step becomes discoverable without weakening deliberate choice or approvals.

**Acceptance.** Positive: keyboard activation from either of two goal cards reaches the same visible heading and blank select, with its label/help announced in the accessibility tree. Negative: navigation alone creates no claim, selects no goal, approves nothing and changes no immutable revision. Edge: after reset, lock/revocation or goals becoming unavailable, old cards and draft generations cannot resume or expose a stale selection; focus falls to a visible safe target. Preserve exact goal content hashes, run/digest identities, export/domain boundaries and unknown publication completion.

**Narrow-width verification contract.** Root owns a native Chromium/Playwright matrix at CSS widths 320 and 390, at actual browser zoom 100% and 200% (read zoom, not device pixel ratio). Capture native-window screenshots, DOM dimensions, accessibility name/focus and Tab order, including long title/owner/acceptance strings. Require no document-level horizontal scroll, clipped controls or obscured focus. Use role/label locators, a negative LiveView state assertion and scoped axe check. Prior 07 images prove only run-card fit (`docs/enhancement-program/iterations/07/verification.md:28-37`). No browser ran for this plan.

**Likely ownership.** One UI author: `portal_live.ex` and focused `goal_claims_test.exs`; root integrates any shared `assets/js/app.js` focus allowlist and `assets/css/app.css` fit adjustment, and alone schedules native browser/Mix checks. Reuse existing navigation and `company-focus` (`portal_live.ex:514-519`; `assets/js/app.js:110-129`).

**Tradeoff and limit.** Correcting confirmed package-name errors that currently receive provider-import advice (`portal_live.ex:425-441,1258-1281`) is smaller and repairs misleading recovery. Continuation has broader workflow value but costs an extra control and needs measured first-time comprehension; prior 4/5 targets were unperformed. Pixel fit and axe cannot establish novice understanding, speech usability or complete WCAG coverage. Company/model fitness and portable authority remain unproven.

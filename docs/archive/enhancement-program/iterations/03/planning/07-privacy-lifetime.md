# Privacy-bound company readiness

**User benefit.** After source removal or backup restore, Company and Packages should explain whether an artifact remains usable. A successful build or evaluation must not remain a green completeness signal after authority is withdrawn. This is a bounded status capability, not a company-coverage claim.

**Current boundary.** [`Company.build_inputs/1`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/company.ex#L139) rechecks exact plan approval and scoped eligibility. [`Harnesses.export/2`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex#L120) repeats writable-workspace and artifact eligibility checks, while [`eligible_binding/1`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex#L146) checks retained plan bindings. [`Privacy.Company.dependencies/2`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/company.ex#L7) identifies dependent plan families and digests; [`purge/2`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/company.ex#L52) removes their bindings and decisions. Source purge deletes affected evaluation runs ([`Purge`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/purge.ex#L339)); unfinished purge returns a blocking state ([`Purge.ready/0`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/purge.ex#L10)). Restore preserves artifact files but clears claim/plan decisions, revokes source connections and interrupts evaluations ([`Snapshot`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/snapshot.ex#L236)); its result explicitly says approvals were not restored ([`restore/2`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/privacy/snapshot.ex#L55)).

**Proposal and interface.** Expose a read-only, freshly computed `Company.readiness(plan_id, artifact_digest)` projection with separate plan authority, artifact integrity/eligibility, evaluation applicability, cleanup and release qualification fields. Return stable reason codes and one safe next action per blocker; render only IDs, hashes and status metadata. Reuse `Company.build_inputs/1`, `Harnesses.eligible/1`, `Evaluations.applicability/2` and [`Releases.Validation.check/3`](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/releases/validation.ex#L8). Compute under the existing privacy fence; do not persist a transferable receipt or source bodies. A restore target remains quarantined until its own fresh approvals and evaluations.

**Acceptance and falsifier.** A currently eligible synthetic package reports its exact identities and any unavailable runtime as blocked. During incomplete purge, readiness blocks. After source purge, affected plan/artifact/evaluation status loses eligibility; unrelated scope retains it. After restore, preserved bytes remain inspectable, but old approvals and evaluations cannot yield “ready.” Changing a claim, plan, model, suite or cleanup state changes the projection at the next read. A single stale “complete” or leaked source body falsifies this proposal. Focused synthetic tests should cover each transition and preserve historical artifact-byte verification.

**Choice and risk.** This gives recovery value without private imports or live model access. Goal-origin policy/conflict admission may deliver more complete instructions; portable approval may serve cross-workspace consumers, but each has broader schema/trust scope ([handoff](../../02/handoff-to-03.md)). A transient status must not become authority: build, export and release retain admission checks.

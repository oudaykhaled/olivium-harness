# 09 proposal: revision-bound multi-source recommendations

**Benefit.** Let an owner select two to four captured documents, plans or history records plus a future goal, receive cited proposed practices, exceptions and conflicts, then edit/reject/approve recommendations and export a company package. Every influencing revision stays visible and revocable. Today `Models.propose/2` sends one source body and `Proposal.validate/2` restricts citations to it (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:150`, `lib/harness_forge/models/proposal.ex:109`). Company plans pin claims and source hashes and recheck them before build (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:243`, `lib/harness_forge/company.ex:328`).

**Connected slice.** Extend the proposal profile for a bounded source set and selected goal, total input bytes and metered output. Capture each `{source_id, revision_id, body_hash}`; authorize active sources and consent categories through `Models.infer/2` (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:89`). Treat bodies as data under server-owned instructions, never as tool calls or approval (`lib/harness_forge/models/profile.ex:3`). Validate citation byte ranges against captured revisions; require two distinct sources for synthesis; transactionally recheck *all* revisions and connections before persisting proposals. Show source/revision, support, disagreement and uncertainty for owner review. Edits create successor proposals; decisions bind exact revisions. Existing plan selection, decision and build/export carry provenance (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:110`, `lib/harness_forge/company.ex:138`).

**Acceptance.** Positive: synthetic policy and exception/history sources plus a future goal yield a cited recommendation; owner edits, approves its successor and a coherent plan, then exports target files binding both current sources. Negative: prompt injection or invented citation grants no authority; malformed output writes no claims; rejected/superseded proposals are absent from export. Edge: revising or revoking either source during inference writes no proposals; later revocation blocks fresh build/export while unrelated sources work. `test/harness_forge/models_test.exs:70`, `test/harness_forge/company_harnesses_test.exs:175`, `test/harness_forge/privacy_test.exs:107` anchor checks. Any stale-source proposal or export falsifies the slice.

**Trade-off.** About 2–3 author-days across validation, Portal review and composition. Per-source batches miss conflicts; autonomous analysis adds authority and cost. A synthetic provider proves workflow mechanics only. Real company/model quality, provider scopes, portable authority and operating-log confidentiality remain unproven; development debug disclosure is a source prediction, not an observed leak (`config/dev.exs:22`).

# Adversarial outcome: one reviewed cross-source synthesis

**Recommendation / ADR.** Context: `Models.propose/2` uses one revision and validates citations against it (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:150`, `lib/harness_forge/models/proposal.ex:88`). `Models.infer/2` already accepts multiple `source_ids` with consent, budget and provider checks (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:92`). Claims permit multiple citations, but goal basis excludes citations (`lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex:289`, `lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex:378`). Plan approval pins claims, goals and snapshots; export rechecks them (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:72`, `lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:134`).

**Decision:** add one *synthesis proposal* action for a selected project, two to five current revisions and one future goal. Send captured excerpts and goal through `Models.infer/2`; require a proposed rule with check/evaluation criterion, historical decision or recurring-failure rationale, exception/conflict or “unknown,” goal linkage, and exact citations from two distinct revisions. Validate ranges and revisions; bound input/output; persist only proposed claims. Portal shows evidence and uncertainty so an operator can edit into a new immutable revision, reject or approve, then compose/export using the exact approval. Stale sources and absent model remain visible. The existing manual claim path stays operator-authored, never an automatic provider fallback.

**Alternatives:** deterministic citation assistance plus human synthesis works offline and is auditable, but costs operator effort. Model synthesis can connect distant history faster, but may conflate discussion with decisions; citation validation cannot establish truth. Keyword summaries are cheaper yet risk shallow theatre.

**Acceptance and falsifier:** A synthetic history includes an adopted rule, later exception, abandoned discussion and future goal. A fixture response yields a reviewable two-source candidate; an edited approval reaches target files while a rejected sibling is excluded. Fabricated range, stale revision, unsupported goal, unavailable model or skipped approval yields no exportable rule. Conflicting current guidance must show both citations and uncertainty. Independent review exercises Portal-to-export and labels fixture inference contract evidence, not real-company quality. Principal Elixir owns synthesis/validation; UI specialist owns review; root owns wiring/export. Three authors, medium effort, one project/goal. Failure to distinguish abandoned discussion from adopted decision falsifies analysis benefit.

# 04 — Legacy eligibility and compiler boundary

**Recommendation: gate new plan approvals on selected-goal milestone coverage, with explicit historical grandfathering.** This is one bounded admission benefit, owned by the Company context. `Validation.plan/1` permits `milestones: []` and `goal_ids: []`; it already validates each supplied milestone’s selected goal IDs and nonempty acceptance/evaluation text ([validation.ex:37](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/company/validation.ex#L37), [validation.ex:79](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/company/validation.ex#L79)). Place a set-coverage check in `Company.approval_eligible/2`, after existing supersession and `eligible/1` checks, before the decision and audit inserts in the same transaction ([company.ex:111](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/company.ex#L111), [company.ex:340](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/company.ex#L340)). Return bounded, sorted uncovered goal IDs; reject remains available. Drafts stay partial.

**Compatibility boundary.** `Company.build_inputs/1` requires an exact approved revision and rechecks current evidence/snapshot, but never calls `approval_eligible/2` ([company.ex:138](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/company.ex#L138)). `Harnesses.build_plan/2`, artifact binding, and export eligibility use that path ([harnesses.ex:10](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex#L10), [harnesses.ex:184](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex#L184)). Thus an admission-only change deliberately preserves previously approved uncovered revisions and their valid artifacts; it does **not** establish universal coverage. Successors are new revisions and must pass the gate even when their approved ancestors remain buildable ([company_test.exs:143](../../../../../../test/harness_forge/company_test.exs#L143), [company_harnesses_test.exs:107](../../../../../../test/harness_forge/company_harnesses_test.exs#L107)). Milestones are in the hashed plan payload, but `Harnesses.specification/3` emits goals/rules, not milestone coverage; package bytes cannot prove the new admission rule ([harnesses.ex:194](../../../../../../lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex#L194)). Do not rewrite old approvals, hashes, bindings, or packages.

**Acceptance:** two selected goals covered by valid milestones approve the exact current revision; one uncovered goal refuses with its ID and leaves decision/audit/artifact counts unchanged; duplicate coverage of one goal cannot cover another. A zero-goal plan retains its existing approval behavior. Superseded proposals still refuse, while an approved uncovered ancestor remains buildable/exportable subject to existing drift and revocation checks. An uncovered **new** approval or a newly blocked valid historical artifact falsifies the contract.

Creation-time enforcement offers a stronger invariant but prevents partial drafting and breaks current fixture journeys. A build/export gate enforces universal coverage but retroactively withdraws approved authority and existing packages. Scope excludes policy completeness, real-company outcome quality, managed runtime and portable recipient authority. Estimated effort: one Company helper, focused transaction/compatibility tests, and fixture updates by the Company owner; no compiler or schema migration.

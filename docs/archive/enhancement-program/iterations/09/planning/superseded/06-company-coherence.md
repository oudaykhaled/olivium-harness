# 09/06 — Company coherence decision

**Recommendation:** choose selected-goal milestone coverage at plan approval. This is a bounded check of *declared accounting*, not a claim that the company's policy is complete or that its rules will work for a real model. The accepted 08 handoff leaves mandatory-obligation coherence and representative owner-reviewed outcomes unproven (`docs/enhancement-program/iterations/08/handoff-to-09.md`).

**Context.** `Company.Validation.plan/1` permits `milestones: []`, while validating each supplied milestone's selected-goal references, acceptance text, evaluation method and acyclic dependencies (`lib/harness_forge/company/validation.ex:37-65,79-113`). `Company.decide_plan/2` checks exact revision, eligibility and audit-atomic insertion, but no selected-goal coverage (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:110-136,340-346`); the test explicitly approves an omitted-milestone plan (`test/harness_forge/company_test.exs:400-405`). Approved inputs then reach `Harnesses` and the deterministic compiler (`lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:70-90`; `lib/harness_forge/compiler.ex:23-30`). `Contracts` already rejects rules referencing absent goals (`lib/harness_forge/contracts.ex:287-321`).

**Decision.** At approval admission, require the union of valid milestone `goal_ids` to contain every selected plan `goal_id`. Return sorted, bounded uncovered IDs on refusal. Keep draft creation, rejection, historical approved revisions, and exact revision/eligibility checks intact. A Principal Elixir engineer owns the small `Company` admission change and focused `company_test.exs` cases; likely one PR, under 100 net lines. Blast radius: plan approval and prospective downstream builds only. No schema or compiler format change.

**Acceptance.** Positive: one valid milestone per selected goal approves the exact current revision. Negative: one uncovered selected goal refuses without decision or audit. Edge: repeated coverage of goal A cannot hide missing B; zero selected goals preserve existing behavior, and stale/superseded revisions still refuse for their current reasons. Falsifier: any approved new plan with an uncovered selected goal, or any new decision/audit after that refusal.

**Alternatives/consequences.** Repository dependency cycles are detectable, but `company_test.exs:98-105` intentionally accepts one; declaring every cycle invalid would impose unagreed domain policy. A broad cross-rule contradiction checker would infer semantic truth from free text and risk false authority. Coverage adds one review-time correction and proves only the relationship users declared. Real-company/model qualification remains separate.

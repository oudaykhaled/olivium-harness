# 04 — Legacy revisions in cross-source analysis

**Recommendation:** add a versioned company-analysis review packet beside `Models.propose/2`, then route accepted suggestions through existing claim/plan authority. The legacy operation accepts one active latest revision and validates citations against that revision (`lib/harness_forge/models.ex:150–184`; `lib/harness_forge/models/proposal.ex:100–124`). Changing its schema in place would make existing single-source callers ambiguous. Reuse budget, consent and inference controls with an explicit bounded list of pinned source/revision pairs and a separate strict schema.

The packet distinguishes recurring practice, failure/exception, decision versus discussion, changed/conflicting guidance, and proposed future rule/skill/check with evaluation criterion. Each factual suggestion carries exact byte citations and uncertainty; conflicting guidance stays visible. Evidence already accepts multiple citations on one proposed claim (`lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex:289–318,430–452`). Every citation must still point to a latest active revision (`lib/harness_forge/evidence.ex:561–570`). Recheck all pinned revisions after inference; a stale member blocks the packet. Historical text may inform interpretation but cannot become an approvable current citation.

The portal supports claim proposal and exact-revision decision (`lib/harness_forge_web/live/portal_live.ex:344–419`); the Company panel supports proposed plans, separate decisions and builds (`lib/harness_forge_web/components/company_panel.ex:330–435`). Offer an editable candidate from reviewed claims and explicit future goals, preserving human edit/reject/approve and Company scope checks (`lib/harness_forge/company.ex:234–265,340–346`). Build/export recheck binding and current authority (`lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:10–25,122–170,184–192`). Previously approved uncovered plans and immutable packages retain their original eligibility; successors need a fresh decision. Compiler output contains goals/rules, not history analysis or milestones (`lib/harness_forge/harnesses.ex:194–266`); do not label the exported artifact as enforcing those.

**Acceptance:** two current synthetic sources with contradictory practice yield cited, uncertainty-marked suggestions; operator edits one, rejects another, approves the edited revision/plan, then exports target files containing only approved guidance. Invalid citation or changed source blocks admission; rejection grants no authority. A historical approved package stays buildable while its original evidence remains current. An uncited fact treated as authority, stale-source approval, or legacy package invalidated solely by the new workflow falsifies the design.

Owners: model/schema, portal review, Company composition/tests; at most three authors, no migration/compiler change. Medium effort. A plain multi-source summary is cheaper but cannot deliver reviewed package authority. Synthetic success does not prove real-company semantic quality, connector scope, managed runtime or portable recipient authority.

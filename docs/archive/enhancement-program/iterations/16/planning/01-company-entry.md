# Company entry: make the active corpus route legible

**User and job.** A company owner with an authorised corpus, saved project and goal needs the next valid step toward a reviewed harness without guessing whether manual source assignment is required. Success is four of five first-time operators taking the correct next action unaided in a public walkthrough. An ineligible approval/build action or silent project/revision substitution falsifies the hypothesis.

**Observed problem.** The iteration 15 Company entry shows “Build from company evidence,” then a second workflow region, “0 assigned current captured sources,” seven prerequisite steps, and another “Find evidence” button. That state had zero manual assignments and a saved goal; the journey later captured four files, reviewed contrary history, approved a corrected claim and separate plan, and exported eight files. This can imply assignment is prerequisite, although `Guide.eligible_claim?/5` exempts corpus analysis version 2. This is an inference, not observed novice failure. [Entry snapshot](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/15/browser-evidence/01-company-entry.txt); [Guide snapshot](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/16/source-safe/lib/harness_forge_web/components/company/guide.ex); [handoff](../../15/handoff-to-16.md).

**Options and decision.** Option A reorders Company entry and Guide around one project-scoped corpus action, with manual two-to-four-source setup disclosed on demand. It reuses `company_corpus_open`, `company_guide_open` and saved project selection. It is small, but must avoid equating readiness with semantic coverage. Option B adds a cross-stage dashboard. It may reduce later navigation, but adds state joins and stale-status risk. Recommend A. Name-field validation is a separate handoff concern.

**Acceptance and Root verification.** Given a saved project and goal with zero manual assignments, when Company opens, then its primary action reaches that project's corpus review and says assignment is optional. Given multiple projects or unavailable records, when Company opens, then it requests project choice or shows unavailability. Given an unsaved draft, when corpus navigation is attempted, then the existing guard preserves the draft and prevents navigation. Given an approved claim but no approved plan, when the owner returns, then the next action points to separate plan proposal/review, never export. Root verifies these through the real WEB UI, including accessible names, keyboard focus, 390×844 layout and changed-scope recovery; inspect exact claim/plan hashes and export/evaluation states.

**Authority and limits.** Navigation projects server-owned saved records, currentness and preflight; it grants no approval or completeness claim. Static integrity remains separate from task outcome. Scripted synthesis and blocked task do not qualify semantic quality, novice success, physical scale or own-MCP context. The outcome metric remains owner-reviewed task correctness and obligation coverage with zero critical violations.

# Goal and historical context at the review decision

**User and job.** A company owner reviewing a proposed harness rule needs to decide whether captured history supports a current obligation and whether a saved future goal calls for new guidance. Success is an owner accurately distinguishing current control, scoped exception, abandoned discussion and future intent before approving an exact claim. A promoted discussion or a goal treated as approval falsifies the hypothesis.

**Observed problem.** Iteration 15’s public journey retrieved the governing policy and contrary exception without manual assignments, yet its scripted first proposal only required “a reviewer”; the operator supplied the two ordinary reviewers, scoped emergency exception and successful rollback [product retrospective](../../15/retro/product.md). The actual task evaluation remained BLOCKED with no selected model [handoff](../../15/handoff-to-16.md). The current WEB review already shows saved goal owner and acceptance, captured document status/effective/event dates, exact context and original findings, but separates them across scope, metadata, results and report sections [corpus review UI](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/16/source-safe/lib/harness_forge_web/components/company/corpus_review.ex). Captured historical metadata is optional and explicitly whitelisted; connector status and capture order do not confer documentary authority [corpus review authority](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/16/source-safe/lib/harness_forge/company/corpus_review.ex). The synthesis prompt correctly says future goals do not override existing controls [analysis v2](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/16/source-safe/lib/harness_forge/company/analysis_v2.ex).

**Bounded options.** (1) Reuse the existing corpus review report to add a compact, read-only “basis for this decision” summary: saved goal as future intent; selected historical passages with captured status/date or “unknown”; contrary and unknown findings; exact-context links; then the existing human resolution action. This reduces context switching at the consequential decision, but adds density on a narrow screen. (2) Put a similar summary in the Company guide before plan creation. It would aid the handoff and reuse saved plan/claim identities [guide and plan](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/16/source-safe/lib/harness_forge_web/components/company/guide.ex), but arrives after the claim decision and risks implying that plan approval repairs weak evidence. Recommend option 1 with progressive disclosure of full excerpts. Keep the corpus/goal route primary and the separate exact plan decision intact.

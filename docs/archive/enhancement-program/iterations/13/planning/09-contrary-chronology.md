## Connected change and narrow interfaces

For one selected larger authorised corpus and saved goal, run bounded, server-scoped passage retrieval in **four deliberate lanes**: likely support; explicit negation/exception/changed-policy terms; relevant decisions and incidents across time; goal obligations with no located evidence. Return a `RetrievalCase` with `scope_generation`, `goal_hash`, `query_plan_version`, `searched_domains/time_ranges`, `index_coverage`, `truncation`, and arrays of `EvidencePassage` `{source_id, revision_id, body_hash, start_byte, end_byte, parent_window, document_status, event_at, effective_at, captured_at, relation: support|contrary|unknown}`. Relation is provisional, and each field may be unknown; capture time never ranks policy authority. A bounded second pass targets missing critical domains or older exceptions.

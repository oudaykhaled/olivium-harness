Its fixed table/dependency lists predate passage, graph and cached-summary stores ([purge.ex:7–8](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/privacy/purge.ex#L7)). Pinned Graphiti removes episode edges/nodes, then rebuilds surviving summaries; a rebuild exception restores `old_summary`, logs, and is swallowed, so a successful `remove_episode` return cannot authorize serving ([graphiti.py:2174–2251](../../../../../../research/harnessforge-graph-rag-study-2026-11-02/sources/graphiti/graphiti_core/graphiti.py#L2174)).

**Connected change and narrow interface.** Add a durable Elixir-owned source-generation tombstone and reverse dependency manifest covering exact source/revision/episode/passages, cited and uncited prompt inputs, graph facts, transitive summaries, indexes, caches, analyses, claims, plans, artifacts and queued work. In one serialised local transaction, revoke access, record the tombstone and affected closure, and cancel queued dispatch **before** requesting Graphiti removal. Every retrieval, worker dispatch/result, review, approval, build and export checks the current generation and fence; an incomplete or uncomputable closure conservatively denies the relevant corpus/project. Persist per-episode cleanup/checkpoints so restart retries are idempotent. A worker returns removal and per-affected-summary verification results; Elixir clears the fence only after independent surviving-input/revision checks, index/cache invalidation and file/database cleanup succeed. An old Graphiti summary remains blocked even when upstream returns normally. Rebuild uses only surviving authorised inputs under separately consented, metered model attempts. Restore enters quarantine with fresh identity and no inherited consent/approval; retained exports/provider copies/backups remain outside purge guarantees ([privacy.ex:3–11](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/privacy.ex#L3), [SCALABILITY_REUSE.md](../../../SCALABILITY_REUSE.md)).

**Reuse and acceptance.** Reuse `Privacy.Purge`'s durable prepare/resume/ready path and company dependency traversal, extending its explicit lists; use pinned Graphiti 0.31.2 `remove_episode`, rather than a second graph deletion engine ([REUSE-DECISION.md](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/REUSE-DECISION.md)). Positive: a larger authorised corpus plus saved goal yields support/contrary/unknown passages, an edited/rejected recommendation, exact claim and separate plan approval, corrected actual export, and representative evaluation. Negative: revoked or newly contradictory sources cannot influence retrieval, model prompts, approval or export; foreign/empty scope refuses. Recovery: inject deletion success with summary-rebuild failure, process interruption and restored backup; affected output stays denied across restart until verified repair. Freeze oracle, questions, tasks, thresholds and FTS5 baseline plan independently before implementation; compare candidates afterward. Scripted responses prove mechanics only ([handoff-to-13.md](../../12/handoff-to-13.md)).

**Trade-off and effort.** A conservative corpus fence can temporarily block unaffected work, but prevents stale summary leakage when lineage is incomplete; narrow it after a complete reverse index is verified. Estimate 2–3 engineer-days for lifecycle contracts and fault-injection tests, plus backend-specific verification.

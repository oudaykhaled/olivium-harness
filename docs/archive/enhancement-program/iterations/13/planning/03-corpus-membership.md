# Corpus membership and generations: connected proposal

**Observed gap.** Profile/project `source_ids` are payload arrays, validated at 100 IDs, then merged into the allowed-source set for plan resolution; the plan rechecks only referenced revisions against that set and current source state ([validation.ex:7–31](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company/validation.ex#L7), [validation.ex:159–165](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company/validation.ex#L159), [company.ex:245–285](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company.ex#L245), [company.ex:311–337](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company.ex#L311)). That cannot represent a selected large corpus or invalidate a recommendation when a *new* contradictory member arrives without changing its cited revisions. The prior round still admitted only 2–4 whole documents, and inspected extra passages remained outside the report origin ([handoff-to-13.md:13–19](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L13), [handoff-to-13.md:39](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L39)).

**Connected change.** Propose a workspace-scoped corpus identity with relational membership rows keyed by corpus/source and a monotonic corpus generation. Membership mutations use bounded batches, idempotent operations, an indexed keyset metadata page, per-file outcome/checkpoint, and a transactionally advanced generation. A saved profile/project selects a corpus reference; existing array records retain v1 interpretation until explicitly migrated. The server derives a nonempty permitted intersection of corpus membership, active current revisions and connector scope before retrieval or dispatch. No form or prompt carries the entire ID set. A retrieval snapshot pins membership generation, goal hash, source/revision/hash/exact UTF-8 passage spans, parent dates/status, index and Graphiti generation, plus cited, examined-uncited and transitive dependencies. Represent consent as a server-owned pageable dependency manifest and validate its full closure at each actual dispatch; a 100-ID array cannot authorize a larger hidden prompt ([STUDY.md:159–174](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/STUDY.md#L159), [STUDY.md:197](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/STUDY.md#L197)).

**Freshness rule.** Advance generation on source addition, removal, recapture, scope/status change and relevant index rebuild. Conservatively mark *all* analyses, approvals and plans for that selected corpus/project stale on a membership addition until a new retrieval checks for contrary evidence; unchanged cited hashes are insufficient. At review, build and export, compare the pinned generation, revalidate dependency closure and require a new exact claim decision plus separate exact plan approval for a changed recommendation. Keep prior immutable records auditable. Narrowing to per-domain generations can follow measured dependency coverage. Tombstone revoked source derivatives before Graphiti removal and deny serving until cleanup succeeds ([STUDY.md:172–177](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/STUDY.md#L172), [REUSE-DECISION.md:53–63](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/REUSE-DECISION.md#L53)).

**Reuse and acceptance.** Elixir/SQLite remains membership, evidence, consent and approval authority; FTS5 is the baseline. Feed revision-pinned bounded passages to pinned Graphiti 0.31.2's episode/temporal/hybrid routes, with its native Jev routes limited to dedupe; select backend and generative model separately ([REUSE-DECISION.md:15–25](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/REUSE-DECISION.md#L15), [REUSE-DECISION.md:27–39](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/REUSE-DECISION.md#L27)). Demonstrate >100 assigned synthetic sources and resumed >500-file capture; corpus+saved goal yields support, contrary or abstention, a corrected/rejected claim, exact approvals, corrected actual target bytes and paired representative evaluation. Reject empty/foreign scope, forged generation, stale consent, newly added contradiction and revoked source; recover from interrupted batch and failed derivative rebuild without serving stale output. Compare FTS5/candidate on frozen critical-evidence oracles. Estimated membership seam: 3–5 engineer-days, excluding backend/model qualification. Generation-wide invalidation costs recomputation but prevents silent contrary-evidence misses; 51 offline tests and million logical records prove neither physical ingestion nor semantic reliability ([SCALABILITY_REUSE.md:29–32](../../../SCALABILITY_REUSE.md#L29), [handoff-to-13.md:63–71](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L63)).

# Connected product falsifiers and minimum useful slice

**Observed gap.** `Company.Analysis` accepts 2–4 selected whole sources, ≤32,768 body bytes and a ≤49,152-byte prompt; this prevents one goal question spanning an authorised corpus ([analysis.ex:13–40](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company/analysis.ex#L13), [analysis.ex:169–211](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company/analysis.ex#L169)). Its validator checks exact UTF-8 citations and permits unknown/abstention, but explicitly proves provenance rather than semantic truth ([company_analysis.ex:1–3](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/models/company_analysis.ex#L1), [company_analysis.ex:117–159](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/models/company_analysis.ex#L117)). Iteration 12 proved supervised correction and an actual eight-file export; critical-obligation discovery and task improvement remain unproved ([handoff-to-13.md:21–39](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L21)).

**Connected change.** Select one authorised corpus and saved future goal. Capture beyond one 500-file/16-MiB batch with checkpoints, per-file outcomes and partial-coverage state; assign >100 sources through server-owned membership, page metadata and open bounded exact passages. For a goal question, return **support, contrary, unknown or abstain** with source status, effective date, parent context and exclusions. A versioned origin pins corpus/goal/scope generations, index/chunker versions, revision/hash/byte spans and cited, uncited and transitive prompt influences. Feed bounded passages into the existing recommendation review: reject unsafe guidance, edit a successor, approve its exact claim, separately approve the plan, then inspect fresh corrected target exports. Keep Elixir/SQLite/FTS authoritative and reuse pinned Graphiti 0.31.2/Jev through a bounded adapter. Native Jev routes serve node/edge dedupe only; extraction/summaries need a separately supplied generative model ([SCALABILITY_REUSE.md:7–26](../../../SCALABILITY_REUSE.md#L7), [REUSE-DECISION.md](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/11/scale-reuse-steering-01/REUSE-DECISION.md)).

**Acceptance/falsifiers.** Freeze a public corpus, goal, expected support/contrary/outdated/missing passages, critical obligations and representative tasks first. Positive: physical capture resumes after interruption; retrieval finds a critical exception; citations match current parent bytes; corrected wording appears and rejected wording is absent from actual exports. Negative: empty/foreign scope, forged span, goal or uncited-source drift, new contradiction and revocation deny dispatch, approval or export. Failed graph-summary rebuild keeps derivatives unservable. Recovery: incomplete coverage remains visible; refresh requires a new origin and review; insufficient evidence abstains. Compare FTS5 and Graphiti on identical questions for critical misses, citation/context accuracy, abstention, latency and cost. Compare baseline and generated-harness tasks under an independent frozen rubric; current assessments are owner testimony ([assessments.ex:1–8](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company/assessments.ex#L1), [handoff-to-13.md:63–71](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L63)). Any missed critical contrary evidence, stale approval or unsupported critical export fails reliability; no task gain fails usefulness.

**Effort/limits.** Estimate one focused functionality round spanning corpus, retrieval/origin, review/export and evaluation; Astra selects schema/backend in an ADR. Lexical retrieval is cheaper but may miss cross-source relations; Graphiti adds worker/backend/model cost. Fifty-one offline tests and a million-*logical*-document probe do not prove installed behavior, semantic quality or million physical-file capture ([SCALABILITY_REUSE.md:28–32](../../../SCALABILITY_REUSE.md#L28)). Retain iteration 12's lower-pane P2, reopen P3, 496-query mount samples, two baseline Dialyzer diagnostics and 15 Docker skips ([handoff-to-13.md:25–39](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L25)).

# Adversarial minimality: one authorised corpus to corrected export

**Observed gap.** Today's analysis accepts exactly 2–4 selected source references, loads their complete bodies, caps them at 32,768 bytes, and makes one 4,096-token model request; the origin validator independently fixes the same 2–4-source shape. Raising a form limit would leave both gates intact and still omit later contrary evidence ([analysis.ex:169–211](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company/analysis.ex#L169-L211), [origin.ex:84–90](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/evidence/origin.ex#L84-L90)). The 12 handoff confirms inspected passages do not enter the original recommendation, despite real correction and export proof ([handoff-to-13.md:7–21](../../12/handoff-to-13.md#L7-L21)).

**Proposed narrow contract, subject to Astra's ADR.** Define one server-owned `CorpusRun` boundary: `(authorised membership snapshot, saved goal hash, capture generation, bounded passage budget)` in; `(complete/incomplete scan status, support/contrary/unknown passage references, abstention reason, dependency digest)` out. Membership is relational and paged; capture is checkpointed with per-file outcomes, so a failed 501st file can resume without replaying 500 or transmitting a whole-corpus array. The retrieval adapter receives a nonempty server-derived scope and returns candidate IDs only; Elixir reopens current revisions and validates exact UTF-8 spans, parent context, status and dates before prompt or display. A version-2 analysis origin records every cited, uncited and transitive prompt influence plus corpus/goal/config generations. Retain version-1 validation unchanged. A changed source, new contrary passage, revoked membership or failed purge invalidates review/build/export eligibility until rerun; tombstone before Graphiti removal and keep the fence through failed rebuild. Keep current claim edit/reject/exact approval, separate plan approval and deterministic target export ([analysis.ex:47–75,276–342](../../../../../../tmp/enhancement-program/13/source-before/lib/harness_forge/company/analysis.ex#L47-L75), [SCALABILITY_REUSE.md:25–39](../../../SCALABILITY_REUSE.md#L25-L39)).

**Reuse and seams.** SQLite/FTS5 is the measurable baseline and authority. Route a bounded Python adapter to pinned Graphiti 0.31.2 for episodes, hybrid retrieval and native Jev node/edge dedupe. Its `LLMRuntime` routes prompts by model but directly calls the selected client; it does not enforce HarnessForge's per-attempt consent/budget ledger ([llm_runtime.py:147–193](../../../../../../research/harnessforge-graph-rag-study-2026-11-02/sources/graphiti/graphiti_core/llm_client/llm_runtime.py#L147-L193)). Therefore the adapter must deny unpinned stages, reserve each actual retry, and reconcile cancellation/ambiguous billing. Extraction/summaries require a separately supplied generative model. Root owns unavoidable shared migration/config/router and integration seams; two bounded authors own corpus/retrieval and origin/model-control respectively, including their tests. No schema or graph backend is preselected.

**Smallest falsifiable acceptance.** On one public synthetic corpus exceeding 500 physically captured files and 100 memberships, recover an interrupted capture; compare frozen FTS and Graphiti questions for critical support, contradiction, outdated/unknown coverage, exact citations and abstention. Drive one saved goal through rejected and edited proposals, exact claim and separate plan approval, then inspect fresh corrected target bytes and a representative baseline comparison. Foreign/empty scope, forged span, new contradiction, stale consent, retry-budget exhaustion and failed graph-summary cleanup must deny serving or approval. Offline 51 passes and the million-*logical*-record probe prove neither installed backend nor semantic/company/task quality; absent authorised model/backend, report that gate unavailable ([SCALABILITY_REUSE.md:41–48](../../../SCALABILITY_REUSE.md#L41-L48)).

**Trade-off and effort.** This is roughly 2–3 author-weeks plus root integration and independent verification; backend selection, dependency pinning and failure cleanup are the main risk. Lexical-only export is faster but cannot satisfy the pinned reuse direction; graph-only delivery adds complexity without proving the user journey. Stop scope at one completed vertical path and its falsifiers, with broader semantic rollout gated by measured lift.

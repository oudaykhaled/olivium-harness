# Approval-time coverage for company-analysis plans

**Context.** Company analysis binds 2–4 authorised source revisions to one future goal, preserves uncertainty/conflicts, and requires exact Evidence approval (`company/analysis.ex:169–255,299–340`). A Company plan can select goals and approved claims, yet its milestones may name no goals and approval checks eligibility without goal or milestone coverage (`company.ex:110–135,279–285,345–363`; `company/validation.ex:37–55,79–94`). The portal exposes all three before exact approval (`components/company/plan.ex:584–642`). An unaddressed goal can enter exported target files.

**Decision.** For a proposed plan containing a company-analysis claim, check at exact plan approval that every selected goal has a selected, approved, current company-analysis claim pinned to that goal, and that every selected goal is assigned to at least one milestone with nonempty goal IDs. Show a concise per-goal coverage result and missing IDs at review. Keep drafts and rejection possible; a missing link requires an edited successor and new exact approval. Recheck current claim/source/goal context through existing eligibility; no inference, repinning or automatic decision. This structural gate cannot establish semantic completeness or task success.

**Acceptance.** Root runs a synthetic portal journey: two distinct historical sources plus future goal produce an explained, conflicted recommendation; human resolution/edit, rejected predecessor, approved successor, a complete milestone, exact plan approval, deterministic build and target export succeed. Missing goal claim, empty milestone coverage, stale influencing source, forged cross-workspace reference or superseded claim refuse approval; rejected plans stay rejectable; refusal creates no decision/build. Goal/profile/project mutation still invalidates eligibility. Existing approved plans retain build semantics; existing proposed analysis plans may need successors. Public `create_plan`, `decide_plan`, `build_inputs`, export and MCP shapes stay stable; add only a specific approval error and review display.

**Ownership/blast radius.** Two product authors total: principal Elixir owns `Company` coverage check and focused tests; root owns the Company plan display/error integration and all corrections/shared integration. No migration. Strongest alternative: exact owner task/rubric receipt would measure declared outcomes, but needs durable immutable assessment, purge/backup/restore and an explicit separation from trusted `Evaluations` (`evaluations.ex:1–16,63–95`; `privacy/snapshot.ex:7–8,286–339`). Mounted purge invalidation is also eligible, but addresses disclosure freshness rather than this coherence gap.

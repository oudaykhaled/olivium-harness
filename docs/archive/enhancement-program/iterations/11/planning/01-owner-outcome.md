# Owner outcome: assess one real company job

**User/job.** A company owner building emergency-release guidance needs to know whether the exported package helps complete that task while honoring mandatory review, rollback and communication obligations. Success: one owner-reviewed assessment bound to exact package, goal and rubric revisions; **100% of declared mandatory obligations assessed, zero critical violations for accepted success**. One accepted omission or critical violation falsifies this hypothesis (`docs/enhancement-program/PRODUCT_OUTCOME.md:9–21`; `docs/enhancement-program/iterations/10/retro/01-product.md`).

**Gap/recommendation.** Analysis captures 2–4 authorized revisions plus future goal and influencing context; human revisions and exact approvals flow into a separately approved plan and deterministic build (`lib/harness_forge/company/analysis.ex:13–76,195–279`; `lib/harness_forge/company.ex:71–164,232–264,330–366`). Plans allow free-text milestone evaluation; trusted evaluations use fixed `synthetic-go-v1`. Neither yields a company-task result (`lib/harness_forge/company/validation.ex:96–107`; `lib/harness_forge/evaluations.ex:1–30,790–802`). Add one **owner-authored task rubric and assessment** in Company: task prompt, mandatory obligations and critical violations traceable to selected goal/milestone and approved recommendations. After build, record observed results and judgment against exact package digest. Label *owner assessment*, distinct from trusted Evaluation.Run/release qualification. Persist immutable successors and audit; cap sizes. Assessment remains optional for building.

**Acceptance.** Given approved multi-source guidance, goal, plan and exported package, when the owner assesses the task, then Company shows exact task/package/goal/rubric and each obligation result, including evidence or “unobserved,” without runtime qualification. Given an unassessed obligation or critical violation, when success is submitted, then refuse success without a successful receipt. Given changed package, rubric, goal or workspace authority, when an old receipt is reused, then preserve history and require new assessment; foreign access, purge/restore and queued stale writes cannot grant success. Native synthetic review shows edited approved guidance in export, assessed obligation, refusal and successor. Scripted inference cannot prove semantic completeness.

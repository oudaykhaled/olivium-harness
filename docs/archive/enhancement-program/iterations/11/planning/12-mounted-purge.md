# Mounted supported-source purge freshness (unselected)

**Benefit and gap.** An owner may purge a source while Company and Packages remain open in two authorised tabs. Today portal purge broadcasts a broad `:company_cache_reset`; the receiver clears every company draft and package control (`portal_live.ex:866–900`). Direct `Privacy.purge_source/2` completion has no mounted notification (`privacy.ex:19–20`; `privacy/purge.ex:240–256,406–448`). The purge plan identifies affected claim families, plan IDs and artifact digests; source purge removes those lineages while leaving unrelated catalog records (`privacy/purge.ex:130–174,320–389`; `privacy/company.ex:7–103`). Supported reload is required for external-writer freshness.

**Recommended scope.** Publish a workspace-scoped, operation-ID keyed event from the domain **only after** the durable source purge reaches `completed`, including affected identities. Each authorised mounted tab rechecks session and workspace, discards affected report/plan/package observations, rotates request/form generations, and reloads scoped records. Preserve unrelated policy and unsaved unrelated drafts. Keep workspace-purge and restore reset semantics. Domain completion covers supported API/reconcile callers; duplicate delivery is idempotent. Existing analysis reset token, async checks and component generation provide fences (`portal_live.ex:520–641`; `company_panel.ex:36–55,104–123`). Notification causes no inference or record write.

**Acceptance.** In a synthetic authorised corpus with affected and unrelated sources, complete a supported domain-source purge while Company and Packages are mounted in two tabs. Without reload, both remove affected recommendations, selected plans, builds and export controls; a queued pre-purge analysis result and stale form submission cannot revive them. Unrelated policy and an unrelated dirty draft survive. A duplicate event changes no records, starts no model request and leaves both views stable. Failed/incomplete purge emits no completion event. A foreign workspace/tab learns nothing and changes nothing. Existing exported files outside the workspace remain outside purge control; privacy copy must say so.

**Strongest alternative.** An immutable owner-reviewed representative-task receipt advances actual outcome measurement more directly, but adds a new persisted assessment, obligation-completeness contract, purge/backup/restore semantics and review burden; owner judgment cannot become trusted `Evaluation.Run` qualification. Choose on observed user value and bounded acceptance, not retrospective vote count. Limit this proposal to root plus at most two product authors, with root owning shared PubSub integration and migration decisions.

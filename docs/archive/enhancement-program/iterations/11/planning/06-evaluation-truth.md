# Evaluation truth and semantic limits

**Recommended slice:** Give an owner a durable assessment of one representative company task against an explicitly authored obligation rubric for one exact approved package and goal revision. The owner records each mandatory obligation as met, unmet, or unassessed, its evidence, any critical violation, interruption/recovery, and an explicit final judgment. Show the receipt beside the package as **owner reported, runtime unqualified**. This connects approved recommendations to representative work without implying model success. `Evaluations` is exclusively a trusted-check path, limited to fixed `synthetic-go-v1` (`lib/harness_forge/evaluations.ex:1–16,790–805`); its `Run` persists trusted artifact/result/execution context (`lib/harness_forge/evaluation/run.ex:5–10`). Keep owner assessments in a distinct table/API and never feed them into release applicability. The synthetic Go grader checks only its two fixed tasks (`lib/harness_forge/evaluation/task_suite.ex:5–6,63–74`). Company output validates exact citation spans and two-source structure, not semantic truth (`lib/harness_forge/models/company_analysis.ex:2,129–154`).

Bind a receipt to task/text digest, approved package digest, goal ID/revision, rubric digest/version, obligation results, critical flags, actor, and time. Snapshot exact references; reject missing, foreign, stale or mismatched inputs. Persist interrupted attempts without success; acceptance requires every mandatory result and no critical violation. A changed task, rubric, goal or package creates a successor, preserving predecessor bytes. Workspace purge/restore must invalidate affected receipt authority and prevent cross-workspace disclosure; external exports remain outside its reach. Implement through a bounded schema, portal review and scoped tests, with at most three product authors including root.

**Falsifiable checks:** A synthetic four-source/goal journey reaches approved export and one task receipt with all mandatory results. Omit an obligation, report a critical violation, cancel mid-assessment, alter any bound identity, or request a foreign receipt: no successful receipt or release qualification. Reopen predecessor and successor: old result remains attributable; neither silently remaps. Native portal checks cover authoring, interrupted recovery and visible unqualified label. Owner assertions remain unverified judgments; scripted provider responses and structural quotes do not prove completeness, real-model semantics, time saved, or managed runtime.

**Strong alternative:** Security's two-mounted-tab invalidation after supported domain-source purge closes a stale disclosure window (`iterations/10/handoff-to-11.md`). It improves privacy freshness sooner, but does not measure whether a package helps company work. Preserve it as an independent candidate, not a vote.

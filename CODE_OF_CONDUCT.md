# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully, welcome different backgrounds and experience,
and give specific constructive feedback. Critique ideas/behavior with evidence.
Respect privacy, credit others' work and accept moderation decisions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in discussions, code review,
issue reports and spaces representing the project.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation or remove access to protect
participants. They should explain decisions privately where possible, act
proportionately, and protect reporters' identities and personal information.

This local candidate has no published private reporting contact or moderation
service. Before public community activity, the owner must name that contact and
responsible moderators. Do not publish sensitive incident details. Pause ordinary
disputes and ask the maintainer to mediate once that channel exists.
